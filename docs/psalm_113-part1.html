Psalm 113

Praise the Lord! You who serve the Lord, praise his name.

The Lord's name should be praised from now and forever.

From sunrise to sunset, we should praise the Lord's name.

The Lord is higher than all nations, and his glory is above the heavens.

No one is like the Lord our God, who lives on high.

He lowers himself to see what's in heaven and on earth.

He raises the poor from the dust and lifts the needy from the trash pile.

He does this to seat them with the leaders of his people.

He helps the woman with no children to have a home and become a happy mother.

Praise the Lord.
