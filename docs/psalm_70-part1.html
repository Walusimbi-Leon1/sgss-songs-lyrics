Psalm 70

God, hurry to rescue me. Hurry to help me, Lord.

Make those who want to hurt me feel ashamed and confused. Make them turn back and be confused, those who wish me harm.

Make those who say "Aha, aha" turn back and be ashamed as they deserve.

May all who seek you be happy in you. And may those who love your rescue always say, "God is great!"

But I am poor and in need. God, hurry to me. You are my helper and rescuer. Lord, don't delay.
