Psalm 52

Why do you brag about the bad things you do, you strong man? God's goodness lasts forever.

Your tongue plans trouble; it's like a sharp razor, slicing with lies.

You love evil more than good, and you'd rather lie than tell what's right. Selah.

You love all words that hurt people, you deceitful tongue.

God will also destroy you forever. He will take you away, pull you out of where you live, and root you out of the land of the living. Selah.

Those who follow God will see this, be in awe, and laugh at that man.

Look, this is the man who didn't rely on God for strength. He trusted in his riches and made himself strong through his evil.

But I am like a green olive tree in God's house. I trust in his mercy forever and ever.

I will praise you forever because you did this. I will trust in your name, because it is good among those who love you.
