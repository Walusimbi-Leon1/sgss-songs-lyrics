Psalm 9

I will praise you, Lord, with all my heart. I will tell everyone about all the amazing things you have done.

I will be happy and celebrate because of you. I will sing songs of praise to you, the Most High.

When my enemies turn and run, they will fall down and be destroyed when you show up.

You have taken my side and defended my case. You sat on your throne and judged what is right.

You have scolded the nations. You have destroyed the evil people. You have erased their name forever.

You enemy, your destruction is over for good. You destroyed cities, but the memory of them is gone.

But the Lord will last forever. He has set up his throne to judge.

He will judge the world fairly. He will treat all people with honesty and fairness.

The Lord will also be a safe place for those who are beaten down. He is a safe place in times of trouble.

Those who know you will trust you. Because you, Lord, have not left those who seek you.

Sing praises to the Lord, who lives in Zion. Tell the people what he has done.

When he investigates bloodshed, he remembers those who died. He does not forget the cries of the helpless.

Be kind to me, Lord. See the trouble I'm facing from those who hate me. You are the one who pulls me back from death's door.

So I can praise you at the gates of Zion. I will be glad because you saved me.

The nations fell into the pit they dug. Their own foot is caught in the trap they hid.

The Lord shows he is fair by the way he judges. The evil person is trapped by their own deeds. Selah.

The evil will be sent to hell, along with all the nations that forget God.

Those in need will not always be forgotten. The hope of the poor will not disappear forever.

Stand up, Lord. Don't let people win. Let the nations be judged by you.

Make them afraid, Lord, so the nations realize they are only human. Selah.
