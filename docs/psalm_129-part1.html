Psalm 129

Many times they hurt me when I was young. Let Israel say this now:

Many times they hurt me when I was young, but they never defeated me.

They plowed on my back like farmers, leaving long furrows.

The Lord is fair and good. He has snapped the ropes of the wicked.

Let everyone who hates Zion be confused and forced to go back.

Let them be like grass on a roof that dries up before it grows.

No one cutting grass picks it up, and no one gathering bundles carries it.

People walking by don't say, "May the Lord bless you." But we bless you in the Lord's name.
