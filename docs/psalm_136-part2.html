Psalm 136 — Part 2

Parts: P1 P2

He let Israel pass through the middle of it, because his mercy lasts forever.

But he defeated Pharaoh and his army in the Red Sea, because his mercy lasts forever.

Thank the one who led his people through the desert, because his mercy lasts forever.

Thank the one who struck down great kings, because his mercy lasts forever.

He killed well-known kings, because his mercy lasts forever.

He struck Sihon king of the Amorites, because his mercy lasts forever.

And Og king of Bashan, because his mercy lasts forever.

He gave their land as an inheritance, because his mercy lasts forever.

That inheritance went to Israel, his servant, because his mercy lasts forever.

He remembered us when we were in a low place, because his mercy lasts forever.

He has saved us from our enemies, because his mercy lasts forever.

He gives food to every living thing, because his mercy lasts forever.

Thank the God of heaven, because his mercy lasts forever.
