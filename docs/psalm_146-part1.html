Psalm 146

Praise the Lord. My soul, praise the Lord.

As long as I live, I will praise the Lord. I will sing to my God while I am alive.

Don't trust in rulers or in any human, because they can't really help you.

When they stop breathing, they return to the earth. On that very day, their thoughts die.

Happy is the one who has the God of Jacob as their helper, whose hope is in the Lord their God.

He made the heavens, the earth, the sea, and everything in them. He keeps his promises forever.

He makes things right for those who are treated badly. He gives food to the hungry. The Lord sets prisoners free.

The Lord opens the eyes of the blind. The Lord lifts up those who are bent over. The Lord loves those who do what is right.

The Lord protects foreigners. He cares for orphans and widows. But he turns the way of the wicked upside down.

The Lord will rule forever, your God, O Zion, for all generations. Praise the Lord.
