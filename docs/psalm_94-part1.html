Psalm 94 — Part 1

Parts: P1 P2

Lord God, you are the one who should punish the wicked. God, you are the one who should punish them. Show yourself.

Rise up, you who judge the earth. Give the proud what they deserve.

Lord, how long will the wicked win? How long will they celebrate?

How long will they say mean things? All those who do evil brag about themselves.

They hurt your people, Lord, and harm those you care for.

They kill widows and foreigners, and murder orphans.

But they say, "The Lord won't see this. The God of Jacob doesn't care."

Listen, you foolish people. You silly ones, when will you become wise?

The one who made the ear, can't he hear? The one who formed the eye, can't he see?

The one who punishes the nations, won't he also correct us? The one who teaches people, doesn't he know everything?

The Lord knows what people think. He knows their thoughts are worthless.

Happy is the person you discipline, Lord, and teach from your law.
