Psalm 130

I cry to you from the depths, Lord.

Lord, hear my voice. Please listen closely to my prayers.

If you, Lord, kept track of our sins, who could stand?

But with you there is forgiveness, so you can be respected.

I wait for the Lord. My whole being waits. I hope in his promises.

I wait for the Lord more than those who watch for morning. Yes, more than they wait for dawn.

Israel, hope in the Lord. With him there is mercy and abundant rescue.

He will rescue Israel from all their sins.
