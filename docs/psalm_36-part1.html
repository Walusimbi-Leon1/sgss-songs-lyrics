Psalm 36

When I think of the wicked, my heart says they have no fear of God.

He thinks he's great in his own eyes, until his sin is seen as hateful.

What he says is sinful and full of lies. He has stopped being wise and doing good.

He plans trouble while lying in bed. He puts himself on a bad path. He doesn't hate evil.

Lord, your love is as high as the heavens, and your faithfulness reaches to the clouds.

Your justice is like huge mountains. Your decisions are like a deep ocean. Lord, you protect both people and animals.

How wonderful is your love, God! That's why people find safety under your wings.

They will be filled with good things in your house. You will let them drink from the stream of your joy.

You are the source of life. In your light we see light.

Lord, keep showing your love to those who know you, and your justice to those with honest hearts.

Don't let proud people walk over me. Don't let the wicked push me away.

Those who do evil have fallen. They are thrown down and can't get up.
