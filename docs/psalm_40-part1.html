Psalm 40

I waited patiently for the Lord. He heard my cry and listened to me.

He pulled me out of a terrible pit, out of the mud. He set my feet on a rock and made my steps steady.

He put a new song in my mouth, a song of praise to our God. Many will see this and be amazed, and they'll trust in the Lord.

Happy is the person who trusts in the Lord. They don't look up to the proud or follow those who tell lies.

Lord my God, you have done so many wonderful things. Your plans for us are countless. If I tried to talk about them all, there would be more than I could count.

You don't want sacrifices and offerings. You opened my ears to hear you. You don't require burnt offerings or sin offerings.

Then I said, "Look, I am coming. The book says this is written about me."

I love to do what you want, my God. Your teachings are deep in my heart.

I have spoken about your goodness to the whole community. I didn't keep my lips closed, Lord, you know that.

I haven't kept your goodness inside me. I have told others about your faithfulness and your saving power. I haven't hidden your love and truth from the community.

Please don't hold back your mercy from me, Lord. Let your love and truth always protect me.
