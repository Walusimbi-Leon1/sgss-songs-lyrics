Psalm 22 — Part 1

Parts: P1 P2 P3

My God, my God, why have you forsaken me? why are you so far from helping me,

and from the words of my roaring?

oh my God, I cry in the day time, but you hear not;

and in the night season, and am not silent.

But you are holy, oh you that inhabit the praises of Israel.

Our fathers trusted in you: they trusted, and you did deliver them.

They cried to you, and were delivered: they trusted in you,

and were not confounded.

But I am a worm, and no man; a reproach of men, and despised of the people.

All they that see me laugh me to scorn: they shoot out the lip, they shake the head,

saying,

He trusted on the Lord that he would deliver him: let him deliver him,

seeing he delighted in him.

But you are he that took me out of the womb: you did make me hope when I was upon my

mother’s breasts.

I was cast upon you from the womb: you are my God from my mother’s belly.

Be not far from me; for trouble is near; for there is none to help.
