Psalm 48

The Lord is great! He should be praised highly in our God's city, on his holy mountain.

Mount Zion is beautifully placed. It is the joy of the whole earth. It sits on the north side, the city of the great King.

God is known in its palaces as a safe place.

The kings gathered together and passed by.

They saw it and were amazed. They were troubled and hurried away.

Fear gripped them there, and pain like a woman giving birth.

You break the ships from Tarshish with an east wind.

Just as we heard, we have seen in the city of the Lord of hosts, in our God's city: God will keep it standing forever. Selah.

God, we have thought about your love in the middle of your temple.

God, your praise goes to the ends of the earth, just like your name. Your right hand is full of what is right.
