Psalm 21

The king will be happy because of your power, Lord. He will be so glad because you saved him!

You have given him what he wanted in his heart. You didn't hold back what he asked for with his mouth. Selah.

You came to him first with good blessings. You put a crown of pure gold on his head.

He asked you for life, and you gave it to him. You gave him long life forever.

He is greatly honored because you saved him. You gave him respect and majesty.

You have made him the most blessed forever. You made him very happy by your presence.

The king trusts in the Lord. Because of the Most High's mercy, he will not be shaken.

Your hand will find all your enemies. Your right hand will find those who hate you.

When you are angry, you will make them like a hot oven. The Lord will swallow them up in his anger, and fire will burn them up.

You will destroy their children from the earth. You will remove their descendants from among people.

They planned evil against you. They thought up a wicked scheme, but they couldn't do it.

So you will make them turn and run. You will aim your arrows at them.

Lord, be lifted up by your own power. We will sing and praise your strength.
