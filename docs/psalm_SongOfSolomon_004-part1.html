Song of Solomon 4 — Part 1

Parts: P1 P2

look, you are fair, my love; look, you are fair;

you have doves’ eyes within your locks: your hair is as a flock of goats,

that appear from mount Gilead.

Your tes are like a flock of sheep that are even shorn, which came up from the washing;

of which every one bear twins, and none is barren among them.

Your lips are like a thread of scarlet, and your speech is comely: your temples are

like a piece of a pomegranate within your locks.

Your neck is like the tower of David built for an armoury,

whereon there hang a thousand bucklers, all shields of mighty men.

Your two breasts are like two young roes that are twins,

which feed among the lilies.

Until the day break, and the shadows flee away, I will get me to the mountain of myrrh,

and to the hill of frankincense.

You are all fair, my love; there is no spot in you.

Come with me from Lebanon, my spouse, with me from Lebanon: look from the top of Amana,

from the top of Shenir and Hermon, from the lions’ dens,
