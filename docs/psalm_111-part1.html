Psalm 111

Praise the Lord! I will praise the Lord with all my heart, in the gathering of good people and in the community.

The Lord's works are great. All who delight in them search them out.

His work is good and wonderful. He always does what is right, and that lasts forever.

He made sure we remember his amazing works. The Lord is kind and full of love.

He gives food to those who respect him. He will never forget his promise.

He showed his people his powerful deeds, so he could give them the land of the nations.

Everything he makes is true and fair. All his rules can be trusted.

They stay firm forever. He does them with honesty and fairness.

He set his people free. He made his promise to them forever. His name is holy and worthy of respect.

Respecting the Lord is the first step to wisdom. Everyone who follows his rules has good sense. His praise lasts forever.
