Psalm 47

Come on, everyone, clap your hands! Shout to God with a shout of joy.

The Lord is the Most High, and he is awesome. He is a great King over the whole earth.

He will put the people under our rule, and the nations under our feet.

He will choose what we will inherit, the best of Jacob's family, whom he loved.

Selah.

God has gone up with a loud cheer, the Lord with the sound of a trumpet.

Sing to God, sing! Sing to our King, sing!

God is the King of the whole world. So sing your songs of praise with thoughtfulness.

God rules over all nations. He sits on his holy throne.

The leaders of the people come together, even the people of the God of Abraham. The defenders of the earth belong to God, and he is lifted up high.
