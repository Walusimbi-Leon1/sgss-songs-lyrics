Psalm 104

Praise the Lord, my soul. My Lord and God, you are so great. You are wrapped in honor and majesty.

You cover yourself with light like a cloak. You stretch out the sky like a curtain.

You lay the beams of your rooms above the waters. You make the clouds your chariot. You ride on the wings of the wind.

You make your angels like spirits. Your servants are like flaming fire.

You laid the foundations of the earth. It will never be moved.

You covered it with the deep ocean like a cloak. The water stood above the mountains.

When you spoke, they ran away. When you thundered, they hurried off.

They go up the mountains. They go down the valleys to the place you made for them.

You set a limit so they cannot cross it. They will not cover the earth again.

He sends springs into the valleys. They run among the hills.

They give drink to every wild animal. The wild donkeys quench their thirst.

The birds of the sky live by them. They sing among the branches.

He waters the hills from his home above. The earth is satisfied with the fruit of your works.

He makes grass grow for the cattle. He makes plants for people to use. He brings food out of the earth.

He makes wine to cheer people up. He makes oil to make their faces shine. He makes bread to give them strength.

The Lord's trees are full of life. He planted the cedars of Lebanon.

Birds make their nests there. The stork makes her home in the fir trees.
