Psalm 31 — Part 2

Parts: P1 P2

I've heard many people spread lies about me. Fear was all around. They got together and planned to kill me.

But I trusted you, Lord. I said, "You are my God."

My life is in your hands. Save me from my enemies and those who chase me.

Smile on me, your servant. Save me because you are merciful.

Don't let me be ashamed, Lord, because I've called to you. Let the wicked be ashamed and silent in death.

Silence those lying mouths that proudly say cruel things against good people.

How great is your goodness, stored up for those who respect you, shown to those who trust you in front of others!

You hide them in your presence from proud people. You keep them safe in your shelter from hurtful talk.

Praise the Lord! He has shown me his amazing kindness in a strong city.

I said in my panic, "I'm cut off from you." But you heard my cries for help when I called to you.

Love the Lord, all you his people, because he protects the faithful and gives the proud what they deserve.

Be brave, and he will strengthen your heart, all you who hope in the Lord.
