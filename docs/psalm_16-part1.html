Psalm 16

Protect me, God, because I trust you.

My soul, you have told the Lord, "You are my Lord. I have no good things to give you."

But I delight in the good people on earth, the excellent ones.

Those who chase after other gods will have more and more troubles. I won't offer them drink offerings of blood, and I won't even say their names.

The Lord is the part I get as my inheritance and my cup. You take care of what I have.

The boundary lines have fallen for me in nice places; yes, I have a good inheritance.

I will praise the Lord, who has given me advice. Even my heart teaches me at night.

I always keep the Lord in front of me. Because he is right next to me, I won't be shaken.

So my heart is happy, and my whole being celebrates. My body will also rest with hope.

You won't leave me in the grave, and you won't let your holy one rot.

You will show me the way of life. Being with you is full of joy. At your side there are happy things forever.
