Psalm 91

Whoever lives in the secret place of the Most High will stay under the shelter of the Almighty.

I'll say this about the Lord: He is my safe place and my fortress. He is my God, and I trust him.

For sure, he will rescue you from the trap of the hunter and from the deadly sickness.

He will cover you with his feathers, and you can trust under his wings. His truth will be your shield and protection.

You won't be afraid of the scary things at night, or of the arrow that flies during the day.

You won't fear the sickness that spreads in the dark, or the destruction that comes at midday.

A thousand may fall beside you, and ten thousand at your right side, but it won't come near you.

You will only watch with your eyes and see the wicked get what they deserve.

Because you have made the Lord, who is my safe place, the Most High, your home,

no bad thing will happen to you, and no sickness will come near your life.

Because he will tell his angels to watch over you and keep you safe in all you do.

They will carry you in their hands, so you won't stub your foot on a stone.

You will walk on the lion and the snake. You will trample the young lion and the dragon under your feet.

Because he loves me, I will rescue him. I will lift him up high because he knows my name.

He will call to me, and I will answer him. I will be with him in trouble. I will rescue him and honor him.

I will give him a long life and show him my salvation.
