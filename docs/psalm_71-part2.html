Psalm 71 — Part 2

Parts: P1 P2

My mouth will show forth your goodness and your rescue all the day;

for I know not the numbers thereof.

I will go in the power of the Lord God: I will make mention of your goodness,

even of your only.

oh God, you have taught me from my youth: and until now have I declared your wondrous

works.

Now also when I am old and greyheaded, oh God, forsake me not;

until I have showed your strength unto this generation,

and your power to every one that is to come.

Your goodness also, oh God, is very high, who have done great things: oh God,

who is like unto you!

You, which have showed me great and sore troubles, will quicken me again,

and shall bring me up again from the depths of the earth.

You will increase my greatness, and comfort me on every side.

I will also thank you with the psaltery, even your truth,

O my God: unto you will I sing with the harp, O you Holy One of Israel.

My lips will greatly rejoice when I sing to you;

and my soul, which you have redeemed.

My tongue also will talk of your goodness all the day long: for they are

confounded, for they are brought unto shame, that seek my hurt.
