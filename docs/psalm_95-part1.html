Psalm 95

Come, let's sing to the Lord. Let's shout happily to the rock who saves us.

Let's come to him with thankful hearts. Let's sing happy songs to him.

The Lord is a great God. He is a great King above all other gods.

He holds the deep places of the earth. The hills get their strength from him too.

The sea belongs to him; he made it. His hands shaped the dry land.

Come, let's worship and bow down. Let's kneel before the Lord who made us.

He is our God. We are the people in his pasture, the sheep he cares for. Today, if you hear his voice,

Don't harden your heart, like when you provoked me, and like that day you tested me in the wilderness.

When your ancestors tested me, tried me, and saw what I did.

For forty years I was sad because of that generation. I said, They are people who stray in their hearts. They have not known my ways.

I swore to them in my anger that they would not enter my rest.
