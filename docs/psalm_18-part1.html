Psalm 18 — Part 1

Parts: P1 P2 P3 P4

I will love you, oh Lord, my power.

The Lord is my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer;

my God, my strength, in whom I will trust; my buckler, and the horn of my salvation,

and my high tower.

I will call upon the Lord, who is worthy to be praised: so will I be saved from mine

enemies.

The sorrows of death compassed me, and the floods of wicked men made me afraid.

The sorrows of hell compassed me about: the snares of death prevented me.

In my distress I called upon the Lord, and cried to my God: he heard my voice out of

his temple, and my cry came before him, even into his ears.

Then the earth shook and trembled; the foundations also of the hills moved and were

shaken, because he was angry.

There went up a smoke out of his nostrils, and fire out of his mouth devoured: coals

were kindled by it.

He bowed the heavens also, and came down: and darkness was under his feet.

He rode upon a cherub, and did fly: yea, he did fly upon the wings of the wind.

He made darkness his secret place; his pavilion round about him were dark waters and

thick clouds of the skies.

At the brightness that was before him his thick clouds passed,

hail stones and coals of fire.

The Lord also thundered in the heavens, and the Highest gave his voice;
