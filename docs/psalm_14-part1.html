Psalm 14

The fool says in his heart, "There is no God." They are rotten, they have done terrible things, no one does good.

The Lord looked down from heaven on people, to see if anyone understood and looked for God.

They have all turned away, they have all become dirty: no one does good, not even one.

Don't all those who do evil know better? They eat my people like bread, and don't call on the Lord.

They were very afraid, because God is with those who do right.

You have shamed the plans of the poor, because the Lord is their safe place.

I wish the Lord would save Israel from Zion! When the Lord brings his people back from being captured, Jacob will be happy and Israel will be glad.
