Psalm 93

The Lord is king. He wears majesty like a robe. The Lord is dressed in strength, and he put that strength on himself. The world is set firm and cannot be moved.

Your throne has been set up from ancient times. You have always existed.

The floods rose up, Lord. The floods raised their voices. The floods lifted their waves.

The Lord above is stronger than the roar of many waters. He is even stronger than the huge waves of the sea.

Your words are completely trustworthy. Holiness is right for your house, Lord, forever.
