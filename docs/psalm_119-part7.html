Psalm 119 — Part 7

Parts: P1 P2 P3 P4 P5 P6 P7 P8 P9 P10

My soul is continually in my hand: yet do I not forget your law.

The wicked have laid a snare for me: yet I erred not from your instructions.

Your promises have I taken as an heritage always: for they are the rejoicing of

my heart.

I have inclined mine heart to perform your rules alway, even to the end.

I hate useless thoughts: but your law do I love.

You are my covering place and my shield: I hope in your word.

Depart from me, you evildoers: for I will keep the commandments of my God.

Uphold me according to your word, that I may live: and let me not be ashamed of my

hope.

Hold you me up, and I will be safe: and I will have respect to your rules

continually.

You have trodden down all them that err from your rules: for their deceit is

falsehood.

You putt away all the wicked of the earth like dross: therefore I love your

testimonies.

My flesh trembles for fear of you; and I am afraid of your judgments.

I have done judgment and justice: leave me not to mine oppressors.

Be surety for your servant for good: let not the proud oppress me.

Mine eyes fail for your rescue, and for the word of your goodness.

Deal with your servant according to your mercy, and teach me your rules.
