Psalm 63

God, you are my God. I will seek you early. I thirst for you. My body longs for you in a dry land with no water.

I want to see your strength and greatness, like I saw you in your temple.

Your love is better than life. I will praise you with my lips.

I will thank you as long as I live. I will raise my hands to honor you.

I will be full and happy inside, like after a good meal. I will praise you with joyful lips.

I think of you when I'm in bed. I ponder you during the night.

You have helped me. So I will be glad under your wings.

I stay close to you. Your hand holds me up.

But those who try to kill me will go to the grave.

They will die by the sword. They will become food for foxes.

But the king will rejoice in God. Everyone who makes promises in his name will be glad. But those who tell lies will be silenced.
