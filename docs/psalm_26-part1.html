Psalm 26

Lord, judge me. I have lived with honesty. I have trusted in the Lord. So I will not fall.

Lord, examine me and test me. Test my heart and my mind.

Your love and kindness are before my eyes. I have walked in your truth.

I have not sat with worthless people, and I will not go with fakes.

I hate the community of evil doers. I will not sit with the wicked.

I will wash my hands to show I am innocent. Then I will go around your altar, Lord.

So I can shout thanks, and tell of all your amazing works.

Lord, I love your house where you live, and the place where your glory dwells.

Do not take my soul with sinners, or my life with violent men.

They do harm with their hands, and their right hand is full of bribes.

But I will walk in my integrity. Save me and be merciful to me.

My foot stands on level ground. In the communities I will praise the Lord.
