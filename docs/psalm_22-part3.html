Psalm 22 — Part 3

Parts: P1 P2 P3

You that fear the Lord, thank him; all you the seed of Jacob, glorify him;

and fear him, all you the seed of Israel.

For he has not despised nor abhorred the affliction of the hurt;

neither has he hid his face from him; but when he cried unto him, he heard.

My thank will be of you in the great community: I will pay my vows before them

that fear him.

The meek will eat and be satisfied: they will thank the Lord that seek him: your

heart shall live for ever.

All the ends of the world will remember and turn to the Lord: and all the kindreds

of the nations shall worship before you.

For the kingdom is the Lord’s: and he is the governor among the nations.

All they that be fat upon earth will eat and worship: all they that go down to the

dust shall bow before him: and none can keep alive his own soul.

A seed will serve him; it will be accounted to the Lord for a generation.

They will come, and will declare his goodness to a people that will be born,

that he has done this.
