Psalm 80

Listen, Shepherd of Israel, you who lead Joseph like a flock of sheep. You who live among the cherubim, shine out.

Before Ephraim, Benjamin, and Manasseh, wake up your power. Come and save us.

Bring us back, God, and let your face shine on us. Then we will be saved.

Lord God of the armies, how long will you be mad at the prayers of your people?

You give them tears to eat like bread. You make them drink lots of tears.

You make us fight with our neighbors. Our enemies laugh at us together.

Bring us back, God of the armies, and let your face shine on us. Then we will be saved.

You took a vine out of Egypt. You drove out the nations and planted it.

You made room for it, and it grew deep roots. It filled the whole land.

Its shade covered the hills. Its branches were like beautiful cedars.

Its branches reached to the sea, and its twigs to the river.

Why did you break its fence? Now anyone passing by picks it apart.

A wild pig from the forest ruins it. The wild animals of the field eat it.

Come back, we beg you, God of the armies. Look down from heaven and see this vine. Care for it.

It is the vineyard your right hand planted, the branch you made strong for yourself.

It is burned and cut down. They die because you frown at them.

Put your hand on the man at your right hand, the person you made strong for yourself.

Then we won't turn away from you. Give us life, and we will pray to you.

Bring us back, Lord God of the armies, and let your face shine on us. Then we will be saved.
