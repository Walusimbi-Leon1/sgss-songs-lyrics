Psalm 68 — Part 3

Parts: P1 P2 P3

in the sanctuary.

The singers went before, the players on instruments followed after;

among them were the damsels playing with timbrels.

Bless you God in the congregations, even the Lord, from the fountain of Israel.

There is little Benjamin with their ruler, the princes of Judah and their council,

the princes of Zebulun, and the princes of Naphtali.

Your God has commanded your power: strengthen, oh God,

that which you have wrought for us.

Because of your temple at Jerusalem will kings bring presents to you.

Rebuke the company of spearmen, the multitude of the bulls,

with the calves of the people, till every one submit himself with pieces of silver:

scatter you the people that delight in war.

Princes will come out of Egypt; Ethiopia will soon stretch out her hands to

God.

Sing to God, you kingdoms of the earth; oh sing praises to the Lord;:

To him that rids upon the heavens of heavens, which were of old;

lo, he does send out his voice, and that a mighty voice.

Ascribe you power to God: his excellency is over Israel,

and his strength is in the clouds.

oh God, you are terrible out of your holy places: the God of Israel is he that gives

strength and power unto his people. Blessed be God.
