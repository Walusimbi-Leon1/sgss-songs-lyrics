Psalm 2

Why do the nations get so angry, and the people plan something pointless?

The kings of the world get ready, and the leaders meet to plan together against the Lord and his chosen king, say,

Let's break their chains apart and throw off their ropes from us.

The one in heaven will laugh. The Lord will mock them.

Then he will speak to them in his anger and upset them because he is very displeased.

But I have set my king on my holy hill of Zion.

I will tell you the message: the Lord said to me, "You are my Son; today I have become your Father."

Ask me, and I will give you the nations as your inheritance, and the farthest parts of the earth as your own.

You will break them with an iron rod; you will smash them like a clay pot.

So be wise now, you kings; learn, you judges of the world.

Serve the Lord with respect, and be glad but with trembling.

Honor the Son, so he doesn't get angry and you are wiped out on your path, when his anger burns even a little. Happy are all who trust in him.
