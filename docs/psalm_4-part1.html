Psalm 4

Listen to me when I call, my God who makes me right: you helped me when I was in trouble; be kind to me and hear my prayer.

Hey people, how long will you keep shaming my honor? How long will you love empty things and chase after lies? Selah.

But know that the Lord has made those who are faithful special to him: the Lord will listen when I call to him.

Respect the Lord and don't sin: talk with your own heart while on your bed, and be quiet. Selah.

Give the right sacrifices and trust the Lord.

Many people say, "Who will show us anything good?" Lord, look at us with your kind face.

You have made my heart happier than when their grain and wine grew.

I will lie down in peace and sleep, because you, Lord, are the only one who keeps me safe.
