Psalm 124

If the Lord had not been on our side, let Israel say:

If the Lord had not been on our side when people attacked us:

Then they would have gobbled us up right away when they got mad at us.

Then the floods would have overwhelmed us, the stream would have washed over our lives.

Then the raging waters would have washed over our lives.

Thank the Lord, who did not hand us over to be chewed up like prey.

We have escaped like a bird from a trap set by bird hunters. The trap is broken, and we are free.

Our help is in the name of the Lord, who made heaven and earth.
