Psalm 18 — Part 4

Parts: P1 P2 P3 P4

that I might destroy them that hate me.

They cried, but there was none to save them: even to the Lord,

but he answered them not.

Then did I beat them small as the dust before the wind: I did cast them out as the

dirt in the streets.

You have delivered me from the strivings of the people;

and you have made me the head of the heathen: a people whom I have not known shall serve

me.

As soon as they hear of me, they will obey me: the strangers will submit themselves

unto me.

The strangers will fade away, and be afraid out of their close places.

The Lord lives; and blessed be my rock; and let the God of my rescue be

exalted.

It is God that avenges me, and subdues the people under me.

He delivers me from mine enemies: yea, you lift me up above those that rise up against

me: you have delivered me from the violent man.

Therefore will I give thanks to you, oh Lord, among the nations,

and sing praises unto your name.

Great deliverance gives he to his king; and shows mercy to his anointed, to David,

and to his seed for evermore.
