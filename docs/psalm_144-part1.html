Psalm 144

The Lord is my strength, and I praise him. He teaches my hands to fight and my fingers to battle.

You are my good, my fortress, my high tower, my savior, my shield, and the one I trust. You help me keep my people in order.

Lord, what is a person that you notice him? Or a human that you care about him?

People are like nothing. Their days pass by like a shadow.

Lord, bend the skies and come down. Touch the mountains and make them smoke.

Send out lightning to scatter them. Shoot your arrows and destroy them.

Reach your hand from above. Rescue me from deep waters and from foreign people.

They speak empty words, and their right hand is a hand of lies.

I will sing a new song to you, God. I will praise you with a harp and a ten-string instrument.

He saves kings. He rescued his servant David from a harmful sword.

Rescue me from foreign people who speak empty words and whose right hand is a hand of lies.

May our sons grow strong like young plants. May our daughters be like polished cornerstones of a palace.

May our barns be full of all kinds of food. May our sheep multiply by thousands in our fields.
