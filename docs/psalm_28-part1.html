Psalm 28

I cry to you, Lord, my rock. Don't be silent to me. If you are silent, I will become like those who go down into the pit.

Hear my pleas when I cry to you, when I raise my hands toward your holy place.

Don't drag me away with the bad people, those who do evil. They tell their neighbors they want peace, but in their hearts they plan harm.

Pay them back for what they do, for their evil efforts. Give them what they deserve for the work of their hands.

They don't care about what the Lord does, or the things he makes. So he will destroy them and not build them up again.

Praise the Lord, because he has heard my pleas.

The Lord is my strength and my shield. I trusted him with my heart, and he helped me. So my heart is very happy. I will praise him with my song.

The Lord is the strength of his people, and he saves his chosen king.

Save your people, and bless those you call your own. Feed them, and lift them up forever.
