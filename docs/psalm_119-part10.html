Psalm 119 — Part 10

Parts: P1 P2 P3 P4 P5 P6 P7 P8 P9 P10

Your word is true from the beginning: and every one of your good judgments

endures for ever.

Princes have persecuted me without a cause: but my heart stands in awe of your

word.

I rejoice at your word, as one that finds great spoil.

I hate and abhor lying: but your law do I love.

Seven times a day do I thank you because of your good judgments.

Great peace have they which love your law: and nothing will offend them.

Lord, I have hoped for your rescue, and done your commandments.

My soul has kept your promises; and I love them exceedingly.

I have kept your instructions and your promises: for all my ways are before you.

Let my cry come near before you, oh Lord: give me understanding according to your

word.

Let my supplication come before you: deliver me according to your word.

My lips will utter thank, when you have taught me your rules.

My tongue will speak of your word: for all your commandments are goodness.

Let your hand help me; for I have chosen your instructions.

I have longed for your rescue, oh Lord; and your law is my delight.

Let my soul live, and it will thank you; and let your judgments help me.

I have gone astray like a lost sheep; seek your servant;

for I do not forget your commandments.
