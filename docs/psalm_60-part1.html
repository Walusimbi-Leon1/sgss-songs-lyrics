Psalm 60

God, you have pushed us away. You have scattered us. You have been unhappy with us. Please turn back to us.

You made the earth shake. You broke it. Please fix its cracks, because it is still shaking.

You have shown your people hard things. You made us drink the wine of shock.

You gave a flag to those who honor you, so they can show it for the truth. Selah.

So that your loved ones may be rescued; save us with your strong hand, and hear me.

God has spoken from his holy place. I will be glad. I will divide up Shechem and measure out the valley of Succoth.

Gilead is mine, and Manasseh is mine. Ephraim is the strength of my head. Judah is my leader.

Moab is my washbasin. I will throw my shoe over Edom. Philistia, shout for joy because of me.

Who will bring me into the strong city? Who will lead me into Edom?

Won't you, God, who pushed us away? And you, God, who didn't go out with our armies?

Help us out of trouble, because human help is useless.

With God we will be brave, because he is the one who will crush our enemies.
