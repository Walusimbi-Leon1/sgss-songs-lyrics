Psalm 75

We thank you, God, we thank you. Your name is near, and your wonderful works show it.

When I gather the community, I will judge fairly.

The earth and all who live on it are falling apart, but I hold up its foundations. Selah.

I said to the foolish people, "Stop acting foolishly." And to the wicked, "Don't lift up your pride:"

Don't lift your pride high. Don't speak with a stubborn attitude.

Because success doesn't come from the east, west, or south.

But God is the judge. He brings one person down and lifts another up.

In the Lord's hand there is a cup of red mixed wine, and he pours it out. But the wicked of the earth will squeeze out the bitter leftovers and drink them.

But I will tell about you forever. I will sing praises to the God of Jacob.

I will cut off the pride of the wicked, but the righteous will be lifted up high.
