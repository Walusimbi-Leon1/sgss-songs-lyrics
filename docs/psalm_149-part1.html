Psalm 149

Praise the Lord. Sing a new song to the Lord. Praise him in the community of his people.

Let Israel be happy in the one who made them. Let the people of Zion be glad in their King.

Let them praise his name by dancing. Let them sing to him with tambourines and harps.

The Lord is happy with his people. He will save the humble and make them shine.

Let God's people be glad in his glory. Let them sing out loud on their beds.

Let them speak high praise of God. Let them hold a double-edged sword in their hand.

They will take revenge on the nations. They will punish the peoples.

They will chain up their kings. They will put their leaders in iron bonds.

They will do what the written judgment says. All his people share this honor.

Praise the Lord.
