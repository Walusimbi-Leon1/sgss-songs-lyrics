Psalm 84

How lovely are your houses, O Lord of hosts!

My soul longs to be there. I even feel faint for the courts of the Lord. My heart and my body cry out for the living God.

Look, the little sparrow has found a home, and the swallow a nest to raise her young, right by your altars, O Lord of hosts, my King and my God.

Happy are those who live in your house. They never stop praising you. Selah.

Happy is the one who gets strength from you, and who keeps your ways in their heart.

As they go through the valley of tears, they make it a spring. The rain fills the pools.

They grow stronger and stronger. Each of them appears before God in Zion.

O Lord God of hosts, please hear my prayer. Listen to me, O God of Jacob. Selah.

Look, O God our shield, and look on the face of your anointed one.

A single day in your courtyards is better than a thousand anywhere else. I'd rather be a doorkeeper in my God's house than live in the homes of the wicked.

For the Lord God is like a sun and a shield. The Lord gives favor and honor. He will not hold back any good thing from those who live rightly.

O Lord of hosts, happy is the person who trusts in you.
