Psalm 15

Lord, who can stay in your house? Who can live on your holy mountain?

The one who lives with integrity, does what is right, and speaks truth from the heart.

He doesn't gossip about others, doesn't harm his neighbor, and doesn't join in blaming his neighbor.

He looks down on a bad person, but he respects those who fear the Lord. He keeps his promise even when it hurts him, and doesn't change it.

He doesn't lend money for interest, and doesn't take bribes to hurt the innocent. Whoever does these things will never be shaken.
