Psalm 123

I lift my eyes to you, who live in heaven.

Look, servants watch their boss's hand. A young woman watches her female boss's hand. In the same way, we watch the Lord our God until he shows us mercy.

Lord, show us mercy, show us mercy, because we are treated with so much disrespect.

We are full of the mocking of those who have it easy, and the disrespect of proud people.
