Psalm 109

God, you are the one I praise. Please don't stay silent.

Wicked and lying people have opened their mouths against me. They have spoken false things about me.

They have surrounded me with hateful words. They fight me for no reason.

I showed them love, but they became my enemies. So I turn to prayer.

They paid me back with evil instead of good. They gave me hate instead of love.

Put a wicked person in charge of him. Let Satan stand at his right side.

When he is judged, let him be found guilty. Let his prayers become sin.

Let his life be short. Let someone else take his job.

Let his children lose their father. Let his wife become a widow.

Let his children wander around and beg for food. Let them look for bread in ruined places.

Let a thief take everything he owns. Let outsiders steal the fruit of his work.

Let no one show him mercy. Let no one care for his fatherless children.

Let his family line end. In the next generation, let their name be forgotten.

Let the Lord remember the wrongs of his ancestors. Let his mother's sin not be erased.

Keep them always before the Lord, so he will wipe their memory from the earth.
