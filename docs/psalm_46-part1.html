Psalm 46

God is our safe place and our strength, always ready to help when we're in trouble.

So we won't be afraid, even if the earth is shaken, and even if mountains fall into the middle of the sea.

Even if its waters crash and foam, even if mountains tremble at the rising waves. Selah.

There is a river whose streams will bring joy to the city of God, the holy place where the Most High lives.

God is right in the middle of that city; it will not be moved. God will help it, and help will come quickly.

The nations raged, the kingdoms were shaken. But God spoke, and the earth melted.

The Lord of armies is with us; the God of Jacob is our safe place. Selah.

Come, look at the works of the Lord, the ruins he has made in the earth.

He stops wars everywhere on earth. He breaks the bows, snaps the spears in two, and burns the chariots in the fire.

Just be still and know that I am God. I will be honored among the nations, I will be honored in the whole earth.

The Lord of armies is with us; the God of Jacob is our safe place. Selah.
