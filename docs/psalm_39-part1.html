Psalm 39

I said, I will watch my ways so I won't sin with my words. I will keep my mouth under control like a horse with a bridle while wicked people are around me.

I stayed completely silent. I didn't say anything, not even good things. And my sadness grew inside me.

My heart was burning inside me while I was thinking. Then I spoke out loud.

Lord, help me know when I will die and how long my life will be. Let me see how weak and short my life is.

Look, you have made my days as short as a hand's width. My whole life is nothing compared to you. Truly, even the best person is completely meaningless. Selah.

Truly, everyone lives like an empty shadow. They worry for nothing. They pile up wealth but don't know who will collect it later.

Now, Lord, what am I waiting for? My hope is in you.

Save me from all my sins. Don't let me become a joke to fools.

I was silent. I didn't open my mouth, because you are the one who did this.

Take your punishment away from me. I am being destroyed by the blow of your hand.

When you scold and correct a person for their sin, you make their strength waste away like cloth eaten by moths. Truly, every person is meaningless. Selah.

Hear my prayer, O Lord, and listen to my cry. Don't stay silent when I cry. I am like a stranger and a traveler with you, just like all my ancestors were.

O spare me, so I can regain my strength before I leave this life and am gone.
