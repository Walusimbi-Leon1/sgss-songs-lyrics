Psalm 82

God stands in the community of the powerful; he judges among the gods.

How long will you judge unfairly, and take the side of the wicked? Selah.

Defend the poor and orphans. Make things right for the hurting and needy.

Save the poor and needy. Pull them out of the hands of the wicked.

They don't know, and they won't understand. They live in darkness. The whole world is falling apart.

I told you: You are gods, and all of you are children of the Highest.

But you will die like regular people, and fall like one of the leaders.

Get up, God, and judge the world, because you will inherit all nations.
