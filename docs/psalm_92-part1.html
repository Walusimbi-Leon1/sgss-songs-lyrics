Psalm 92

It's good to thank the Lord and sing praises to your name, O God Most High.

I want to tell of your love in the morning and your faithfulness every night.

I'll do this with a ten-stringed instrument and a harp, making gentle music.

Lord, you make me happy by what you do. I will celebrate the things your hands have made.

Lord, your works are so amazing! Your thoughts are very deep.

A foolish person doesn't know this, and a silly person can't understand it.

When bad people grow like grass, and those who do evil seem to prosper, it's only so they will be destroyed forever.

But you, Lord, are the Most High forever.

Look, your enemies, Lord, look, they will be destroyed. All who do evil will be scattered.

But you will lift me up and make me strong. You will refresh me like with new oil.

My eyes will see my enemies defeated, and my ears will hear the wicked who attack me get what they deserve.

Good people will thrive like a palm tree. They will grow tall like a cedar tree.

Those who live in the Lord's house will thrive in the places of our God.

They will still do good things even when they are old. They will stay healthy and strong.

This shows that the Lord is good. He is my rock, and he does no wrong.
