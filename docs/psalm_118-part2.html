Psalm 118 — Part 2

Parts: P1 P2

The Lord's right hand is lifted high. The Lord's right hand acts with power.

I will not die, but live. I will tell others what the Lord has done.

The Lord disciplined me hard. But he did not let me die.

Open the gates of what is right for me. I will go in and praise the Lord.

This is the Lord's gate. Good people will enter through it.

I will praise you. You heard me and became my savior.

The stone that the builders rejected has become the most important corner stone.

This is the Lord's doing. It is amazing to us.

This is the day the Lord has made. We will be happy and glad in it.

Please save us, Lord. Lord, please give us success.

Blessed is the one who comes in the Lord's name. We have blessed you from the Lord's house.

The Lord is God, and he has shown us light. Tie the sacrifice with ropes to the altar's corners.

You are my God, and I will praise you. You are my God, I will lift you up high.

Thank the Lord, because he is good. His love never ends.
