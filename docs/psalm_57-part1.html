Psalm 57

God, be kind to me, be kind to me, because I trust in you. Yes, I will hide under your wings until these hard times pass.

I will call out to God, the Most High, to God who does everything for me.

He will send help from heaven and save me from the shame of those who want to swallow me up. Selah. God will send his love and his truth.

I am among lions. I lie among people who are like burning flames. These people have teeth like spears and arrows, and their words are like a sharp sword.

God, be lifted high above the heavens; let your glory be over all the earth.

They set a trap for my steps; I feel down. They dug a pit in front of me, and they fell into it themselves. Selah.

My heart is steady, God, my heart is steady. I will sing and praise you.

Wake up, my soul! Wake up, my harp and lyre! I will get up early.

I will praise you, Lord, among the people; I will sing to you among the nations.

Because your love is as high as the heavens, and your truth reaches the clouds.

God, be lifted high above the heavens; let your glory be over all the earth.
