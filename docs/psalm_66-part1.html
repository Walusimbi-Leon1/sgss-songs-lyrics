Psalm 66

Shout happily to God, all the world:

Sing about how great his name is: make his praise amazing.

Say to God, How awesome you are in what you do! Because you are so powerful, your enemies will give in to you.

Everyone on earth will worship you and sing to you; they will sing about your name. Selah.

Come and see what God has done: he is awesome in how he treats people.

He turned the sea into dry ground: they walked through the sea on foot: we celebrated him.

He rules with his power forever; he watches the nations: don't let the rebellious boast. Selah.

Come on, everyone, bless our God, and let his praise be heard:

He keeps us alive and doesn't let us stumble.

God, you have tested us: you have refined us like silver is refined.

You caught us in a trap; you laid pain on our backs.

You let people ride over our heads; we went through fire and water: but you brought us out to a rich place.

I will go to your house with offerings: I will keep my promises to you,

which I said with my lips when I was in trouble.

I will bring you fat animals as offerings, with ram incense; I will bring cattle and goats. Selah.

Come and listen, all you who respect God, and I will tell what he did for me.

I called out to him with my mouth, and I praised him with my tongue.
