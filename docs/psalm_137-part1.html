Psalm 137

By the rivers of Babylon, we sat down. We cried when we remembered Zion.

We hung our harps on the willow trees there in the middle of that place.

Our captors asked us to sing a song. Those who hurt us wanted us to be happy and said, "Sing us one of the songs of Zion."

How can we sing the Lord's song in a foreign land?

If I forget you, Jerusalem, may my right hand lose its skill.

If I don't remember you, may my tongue stick to the roof of my mouth. If I don't love Jerusalem more than my greatest joy.

Lord, remember the people of Edom when Jerusalem was destroyed. They said, "Tear it down, tear it down, all the way to its foundations."

Babylon, you will be destroyed. Happy is the person who pays you back for what you did to us.

Happy is the one who grabs your little children and smashes them against the rocks.
