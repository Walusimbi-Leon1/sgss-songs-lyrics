Psalm 138

I will praise you with all my heart. Before other gods, I will sing songs of praise to you.

I will worship toward your holy temple. I will praise your name because you are loving and true. You have made your word more important than anything, even your own name.

When I cried out to you, you answered me. You gave me strength in my soul.

All the kings of the earth will praise you, Lord, when they hear what you say.

Yes, they will sing as they follow the Lord's ways, because the Lord's glory is so great.

Even though the Lord is high above all, he cares for the humble. But he keeps his distance from the proud.

Even when I walk through hard times, you will give me life. You will reach out your hand to stop my enemies' anger, and your power will save me.

The Lord will finish what he started in my life. Your love, Lord, lasts forever. Please don't abandon what you have made with your own hands.
