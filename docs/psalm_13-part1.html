Psalm 13

Lord, how long will you forget me? Forever? How long will you turn away from me?

How long must I worry in my mind and feel sad every day? How long will my enemy keep winning over me?

Lord my God, pay attention and listen to me. Brighten my eyes, or I will die.

Or else my enemy will say, "I beat him." And those who hurt me will cheer when I fall.

But I trust in your love. My heart will be glad because you rescue me.

I will sing to the Lord, because he has been so good to me.
