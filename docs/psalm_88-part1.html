Psalm 88

Lord God who saves me, I have cried to you day and night.

Please hear my prayer. Listen to my cry for help.

I am full of trouble, and my life is close to death.

I am counted among those who go down to the grave. I am like a man with no strength.

I am with the dead, like those killed and lying in the grave, whom you no longer remember. They are cut off from your hand.

You have put me in the lowest pit, in dark and deep places.

Your anger weighs heavy on me, and you have crushed me with all your waves. Selah.

You have pushed my friends far away from me. You have made me disgusting to them. I am shut in and cannot come out.

My eyes are sad because of my pain. Lord, I have called to you every day, reaching out my hands to you.

Will you show wonders to the dead? Will the dead get up and praise you? Selah.

Will your love be spoken of in the grave? Or your faithfulness in the place of destruction?

Will your wonders be known in the dark? Will your righteousness be known in the land of forgetting?

But I have cried to you
