Psalm 59

Save me from my enemies, my God. Defend me from those who attack me.

Save me from those who do evil. Rescue me from violent people.

Look, they are waiting to harm me. Strong people are gathering against me. But it's not because I did anything wrong, Lord.

They rush around and get ready to attack, even though I did nothing wrong. Wake up and help me, look!

So you, Lord God, the God of Israel, wake up and confront all the nations. Don't be merciful to any wicked wrongdoers. Selah.

They come back in the evening. They make noise like dogs and roam around the city.

Look, they spew words from their mouths. Their lips are like swords. They say, "Who can hear us?"

But you, Lord, will laugh at them. You will mock all the nations.

I will wait for you because you are strong. God is my protection.

The God of mercy will go before me. God will let me see my enemies defeated.

Don't kill them, or my people might forget. Scatter them with your power. Bring them down, Lord, our shield.

Because of the sin they speak with their mouths and lips, let them be caught in their pride. They curse and lie.

Destroy them in your anger, wipe them out so they are gone. Let them know that God is king over his people everywhere on earth. Selah.

In the evening let them come back. Let them make noise like dogs and roam the city.

Let them wander around looking for food. Let them grumble if they aren't full.

But I will sing about your power. Yes, I will sing loudly of your mercy in the morning. You have been my protection and safe place when I was in trouble.

To you, my strength, I will sing. God is my protection and the God of my mercy.
