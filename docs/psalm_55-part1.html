Psalm 55

Listen to my prayer, God, and don't hide from my cry for help.

Pay attention to me and listen. I am sad and complaining, and I cry out loud.

It's because of my enemy's voice and the wicked hurting me: they accuse me of evil, and in anger they hate me.

My heart aches deep inside, and I'm terrified, feeling like death is near.

I am full of fear and shaking, and horror has taken over me.

I said, "If only I had wings like a dove! Then I could fly away and find rest."

I would go far away and live in the wilderness. Selah

I would hurry to escape the raging storm.

Lord, destroy them and confuse their talk, because I have seen violence and fighting in the city.

They walk around its walls day and night; trouble and sadness are inside it.

Evil is in the middle of it; lies and trickery never leave its streets.

It wasn't an enemy who insulted me—I could have handled that. It wasn't someone who hated me who attacked me; I would have hidden from him.

But it was you, someone like me, my friend and guide.
