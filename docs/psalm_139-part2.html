Psalm 139 — Part 2

Parts: P1 P2

You made my inner parts. You protected me in my mother's womb.

I will praise you, because I am amazingly made. Your works are wonderful, and my soul knows that very well.

My body was not hidden from you when I was made in secret, skillfully formed deep in the earth.

Your eyes saw my body even when it was not yet formed. In your book all my parts were written down, shaped over time before any of them existed.

How precious your thoughts are to me, God! How many there are!

If I tried to count them, they would be more than the sand. When I wake up, I am still with you.

God, you will surely destroy the wicked. So you violent people, stay away from me.

They speak evil against you, and your enemies misuse your name.

Lord, don't I hate those who hate you? Am I not sad about those who fight against you?

I hate them completely. I treat them as my enemies.

God, search me and know my heart. Test me and know my thoughts.

See if there is any evil in me, and lead me on the path of life forever.
