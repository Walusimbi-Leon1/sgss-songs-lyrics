Psalm 99

The Lord is king. Let the people tremble. He sits between the cherubim. Let the earth shake.

The Lord is great in Zion. He is high above all people.

Let them praise your great and awesome name, because it is holy.

The King is strong and loves justice. You make things fair. You judge rightly and do what is right for the people of Jacob.

Praise the Lord our God, and worship at his feet, because he is holy.

Moses and Aaron were his priests. Samuel was among those who call on his name. They called to the Lord, and he answered them.

He spoke to them from the cloudy pillar. They kept his teachings and the rules he gave them.

You answered them, Lord our God. You forgave them, even though you punished them for the bad things they did.

Praise the Lord our God, and worship on his holy hill, because the Lord our God is holy.
