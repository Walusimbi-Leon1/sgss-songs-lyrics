Psalm 119 — Part 1

Parts: P1 P2 P3 P4 P5 P6 P7 P8 P9 P10

Blessed are the undefiled in the way, who walk in the law of the Lord.

Blessed are they that keep his promises, and that seek him with the whole heart.

They also do no wrong: they walk in his ways.

You have commanded us to keep your instructions carefully.

oh that my ways were directed to keep your rules!

Then will I not be ashamed, when I have respect to all your commandments.

I will thank you with uprightness of heart, when I will have learned your good

judgments.

I will keep your rules: oh forsake me not utterly.

Wherewithal will a young man cleanse his way? by taking heed thereto according to your

word.

With my whole heart have I sought you: oh let me not wander from your commandments.

Your word have I hid in mine heart, that I might not wrong against you.

Blessed are you, oh Lord: teach me your rules.

With my lips have I declared all the judgments of your mouth.

I have rejoiced in the way of your promises, as much as in all riches.

I will meditate in your instructions, and have respect to your ways.

I will delight myself in your rules: I will not forget your word.

Deal bountifully with your servant, that I may live, and keep your word.

Open you mine eyes, that I may look wondrous things out of your law.

I am a stranger in the earth: hide not your commandments from me.

My soul breaks for the longing that it has to your judgments at all times.
