Psalm 150

Praise the Lord. Praise God in his holy place. Praise him in the sky where he shows his power.

Praise him for the powerful things he does. Praise him because he is so great.

Praise him with trumpets. Praise him with harps and stringed instruments.

Praise him with tambourines and dancing. Praise him with stringed instruments and organs.

Praise him with loud cymbals. Praise him with crashing cymbals.

Everything that breathes should praise the Lord. Praise the Lord.
