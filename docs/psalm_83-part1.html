Psalm 83

God, don't be silent. Don't stay quiet, and don't just sit there doing nothing.

Look, your enemies are making a lot of noise. Those who hate you are acting proud and bold.

They are making sneaky plans against your people. They are plotting against those you protect.

They say, "Come on, let's wipe them out as a nation. Let's make sure Israel is forgotten forever."

They have all agreed together. They have joined forces against you.

The people of Edom, the Ishmaelites, Moab, and the Hagarenes

The people of Gebal, Ammon, Amalek, and the Philistines along with those in Tyre

Assyria has joined them too. They have helped the descendants of Lot. Selah

Deal with them like you did with the Midianites. Like you did with Sisera and Jabin at the Kison brook.

They were destroyed at Endor. They became like manure on the ground.

Make their leaders like Oreb and Zeeb. Make all their princes like Zebah and Zalmunna.

They said, "Let's take God's houses for ourselves."

My God, make them like a rolling wheel. Like chaff blown by the wind.

Like fire burns a forest, and flames set mountains ablaze;

Chase them with your storm, and terrify them with your wind.

Make them ashamed, so they will look for you, Lord.

Let them be confused and upset forever. Let them be ashamed and destroyed.

Then everyone will know that you, whose name is Jehovah, are the highest over all the earth.
