Psalm 142

I cried to the Lord; I asked him for help.

I told him what was troubling me; I showed him my problems.

When I felt overwhelmed inside, you knew where I was going. On the path I walked, they secretly set a trap for me.

I looked to my right and saw that no one knew me. I had no safe place; no one cared about me.

I cried to you, Lord: I said, you are my safe place and what I have among the living.

Listen to my cry, because I am really down. Save me from those who hurt me, for they are stronger than me.

Set me free from this prison, so I can praise you. Good people will gather around me, because you will be good to me.
