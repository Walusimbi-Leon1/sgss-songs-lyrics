Psalm 116

I love the Lord because he has heard my voice and my prayers.

Because he has listened to me, I will pray to him as long as I live.

I was surrounded by the sadness of death, and the pains of the grave grabbed me. I found trouble and sorrow.

Then I prayed to the Lord: "O Lord, please save me."

The Lord is kind and fair. Yes, our God is full of mercy.

The Lord watches over those who are helpless. I was down, and he helped me.

Rest again, my soul, because the Lord has been good to you.

You saved my life from death, kept my eyes from crying, and my feet from slipping.

I will live in the presence of the Lord here in the land of the living.

I trusted, so I spoke. I was in great pain:

I said in my panic, "Everyone lies."

What can I give back to the Lord for all the good things he has done for me?
