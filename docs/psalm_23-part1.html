Psalm 23

The Lord is my shepherd. I have everything I need.

He lets me rest in green fields. He leads me to calm water.

He refreshes my soul. He guides me on good paths because of his name.

Even when I walk through a dark valley where death is near, I won't fear any bad thing. You are with me. Your rod and staff make me feel safe.

You set a table for me right in front of my enemies. You pour oil on my head. My cup overflows.

Surely your goodness and love will follow me every day of my life. I will stay in the Lord's house forever.
