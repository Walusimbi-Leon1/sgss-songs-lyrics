Psalm 34

I will praise the Lord all the time. I will never stop saying good things about him.

I will gladly tell everyone how great the Lord is. When gentle people hear this, they will be happy.

Come praise the Lord with me. Let's honor his name together.

I looked for the Lord, and he listened to me. He saved me from all my fears.

They turned to him and felt better. They were not ashamed.

I was hurting and cried out to the Lord. He heard me and saved me from all my troubles.

The Lord's angel surrounds those who respect him. He keeps them safe and rescues them.

Try the Lord and see that he is good. Happy is the person who trusts in him.

You who are the Lord's people, honor him. Those who honor him will never be in need.

Even young lions may go hungry. But those who seek the Lord will not lack any good thing.

Come, children, listen to me. I will teach you how to respect the Lord.

Who wants to live a good life? Who wants many happy days?

Don't say evil things. Don't tell lies.

Stay away from bad things. Do good. Look for peace and chase after it.

The Lord watches over good people. He listens when they call for help.

But the Lord is against those who do evil. He will wipe them off the earth so no one remembers them.

Good people cry out, and the Lord hears. He saves them from all their troubles.

The Lord is close to those who are heartbroken. He saves those who are sorry for their sins.

Good people face many hard times. But the Lord saves them from every one.

The Lord protects all their bones. Not one of them is broken.
