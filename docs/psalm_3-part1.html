Psalm 3

Lord, so many people are troubling me! Many are rising up against me.

Many say about me, "God won't help him." Selah.

But you, Lord, are like a shield for me. You are my glory, and you lift my head high.

I called out to the Lord, and he heard me from his holy hill. Selah.

I lay down and slept. I woke up, because the Lord kept me safe.

I won't be afraid of tens of thousands of people who surround me and oppose me.

Rise up, Lord! Save me, my God! You have struck all my enemies on the cheek. You have broken the teeth of the wicked.

Salvation comes from the Lord. Your blessing is on your people. Selah.
