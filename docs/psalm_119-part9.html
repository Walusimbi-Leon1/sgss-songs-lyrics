Psalm 119 — Part 9

Parts: P1 P2 P3 P4 P5 P6 P7 P8 P9 P10

The goodness of your promises is eternal: give me understanding,

and I shall live.

I cried with my whole heart; hear me, oh Lord: I will keep your rules.

I cried to you; save me, and I will keep your promises.

I prevented the dawning of the morning, and cried: I hoped in your word.

Mine eyes prevent the night watches, that I might meditate in your word.

Hear my voice according to your love: oh Lord,

quicken me according to your judgment.

They draw near that follow after mischief: they are far from your law.

You are near, oh Lord; and all your commandments are truth.

Concerning your promises, I have known of old that you have founded them for

ever.

Consider mine affliction, and deliver me: for I do not forget your law.

Plead my cause, and deliver me: quicken me according to your word.

rescue is far from the wicked: for they seek not your rules.

Great are your tender kindness, oh Lord: quicken me according to your judgments.

Many are my persecutors and mine enemies; yet do I not decline from your

testimonies.

I beheld the transgressors, and was grieved; because they kept not your word.

Consider how I love your instructions: quicken me, oh Lord,

according to your lovingkindness.
