Psalm 1

Happy is the person who doesn't follow the advice of the wicked, doesn't stand with sinners, and doesn't sit with those who mock others.

But he finds joy in the Lord's teachings, and thinks about them day and night.

He is like a tree planted by a stream of water. It grows fruit at the right time, and its leaves don't dry up. Everything he does goes well.

The wicked are not like that. They are like dry husks that the wind blows away.

So the wicked won't stand in the judgment, and sinners won't be with the righteous community.

Because the Lord knows the path of the righteous, but the path of the wicked will be destroyed.
