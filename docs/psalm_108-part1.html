Psalm 108

God, my heart is set. I will sing and praise you with all of me.

Wake up, my harp and lyre. I will wake up early too.

I will praise you, Lord, among the people. I will sing to you among the nations.

Your love is great above the heavens. Your truth reaches to the clouds.

God, be lifted high above the heavens. Let your glory be over all the earth.

Rescue those you love. Save us with your power, and answer me.

God has spoken from his holy place. I will be glad. I will divide Shechem and measure the valley of Succoth.

Gilead is mine. Manasseh is mine. Ephraim protects my head. Judah
