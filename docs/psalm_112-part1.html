Psalm 112

Praise the Lord! Happy is the person who respects the Lord and really loves following his commands.

His children will be strong here on earth. The families of the good people will be blessed.

He will have money and riches in his home. And his good life will last forever.

For the good person, light shines in the dark. He is kind, full of mercy, and does what is right.

A good man helps others and lends them money. He handles his own business with wisdom.

He will never be shaken. The good person will be remembered forever.

He won't fear bad news. His heart is steady, trusting in the Lord.

His heart is strong. He won't be afraid until he sees his wish come true against his enemies.

He has shared with others. He has given to the poor. His good life lasts forever. He will be lifted up with honor.

The wicked will see this and be upset. He will grind his teeth and disappear. The wishes of the wicked will come to nothing.
