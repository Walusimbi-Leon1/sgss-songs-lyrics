Psalm 98

Sing a new song to the Lord. He has done amazing things. His right hand and his holy arm have won the victory for him.

The Lord has made his rescue known. He has openly shown his righteousness to the nations.

He has remembered his mercy and his faithfulness to the people of Israel. Everyone on earth has seen our God's rescue.

Everyone on earth, make a happy noise to the Lord. Be loud, rejoice, and sing praise to him.

Sing to the Lord with the harp. Use the harp and a song of praise.

Use trumpets and horns to make a happy noise before the Lord, the King.

Let the sea roar and everything in it. Let the world and all who live in it rejoice.

Let the rivers clap their hands. Let the hills be happy together.

Before the Lord, because he comes to judge the earth. He will judge the world with righteousness, and the people with fairness.
