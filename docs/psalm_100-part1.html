Psalm 100

Shout with joy to the Lord, all the earth.

Serve the Lord with joy. Come to him singing.

Know that the Lord is God. He made us; we didn't make ourselves. We are his people, the sheep of his pasture.

Enter his gates with thanks, and his courts with praise. Thank him and praise his name.

The Lord is good. His love never ends, and his truth lasts for all generations.
