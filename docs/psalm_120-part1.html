Psalm 120

When I was in trouble, I cried to the Lord, and he listened to me.

Lord, save me from people who lie and from those who speak deceitfully.

What should be given to you? What should be done to you, you liar?

You'll get sharp arrows from the strong, and burning coals of juniper.

I'm so sad that I live far away in Mesech, in the tents of Kedar!

I've lived a long time with someone who hates peace.

I want peace, but when I talk, they want war.
