Psalm 128

Happy is everyone who respects the Lord and follows his ways.

You will enjoy the food you worked for. You will be happy, and life will go well for you.

Your wife will be like a healthy vine by your house, full of fruit. Your children will be like little olive trees around your table.

Look, that's how a person who respects the Lord will be happy.

The Lord will bless you from Zion. You will see good times in Jerusalem every day of your life.

Yes, you will see your grandkids, and peace in Israel.
