Psalm 67

God, be kind to us and bless us. Let your face shine on us. Selah.

Then your way will be known on earth. Your saving help will be known among all nations.

Let the people praise you, God. Let all the people praise you.

Let the nations be glad and sing for joy. You will judge the people fairly. You will rule the nations on earth. Selah.

Let the people praise you, God. Let all the people praise you.

Then the earth will give its crops. God, our own God, will bless us.

God will bless us. Everyone everywhere will respect him.
