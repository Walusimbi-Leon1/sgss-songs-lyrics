Psalm 69 — Part 3

Parts: P1 P2 P3

Pour out your indignation upon them, and let your wrathful anger take hold of

them.

Let their habitation be desolate; and let none live in their tents.

For they persecute him whom you have smitten; and they talk to the grief of those whom

you have wounded.

Add wrong to their wrong: and let them not come into your goodness.

Let them be blotted out of the book of the living,

and not be written with the righteous.

But I am poor and sad: let your rescue, oh God, set me up on high.

I will thank the name of God with a song, and will magnify him with thanksgiving.

This also will please the Lord better than an ox or bullock that has horns and

hoofs.

The humble will see this, and be glad: and your heart will live that seek God.

For the Lord hears the poor, and despises not his prisoners.

Let the heaven and earth thank him, the seas, and every thing that moves in it.

For God will save Zion, and will build the cities of Judah: that they may live there,

and have it in possession.

The seed also of his servants will inherit it: and they that love his name will live

therein.
