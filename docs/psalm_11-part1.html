Psalm 11

I trust in the Lord. Why do you tell me to flee like a bird to the mountains?

See, the wicked are bending their bows. They put their arrows on the string to secretly shoot at good-hearted people.

If the foundations are destroyed, what can the good people do?

The Lord is in his holy temple. The Lord’s throne is in heaven. His eyes watch, and he tests the people.

The Lord tests the good people, but he hates the wicked and those who love violence.

He will rain down traps, fire, sulfur, and a terrible storm on the wicked. That will be their share.

For the Lord is righteous and loves what is right. He looks with favor on those with honest hearts.
