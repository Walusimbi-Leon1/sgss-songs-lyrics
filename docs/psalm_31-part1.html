Psalm 31 — Part 1

Parts: P1 P2

I trust you, Lord. Please don't let me be ashamed. Save me because you are righteous.

Listen to me, Lord. Hurry and save me. Be my strong rock, a safe place to protect me.

You are my rock and my fortress. So lead me and guide me, for the sake of your name.

Pull me out of the trap they set secretly for me, because you are my strength.

I put my life in your hands. You have rescued me, Lord, faithful God.

I hate those who trust in worthless lies, but I trust in the Lord.

I will be happy and rejoice in your kindness, because you have seen my trouble and known my struggles.

You didn't hand me over to my enemy. You gave me room to breathe.

Have mercy on me, Lord, because I'm in trouble. My eyes are worn out from sadness, and so are my soul and body.

My life is full of sorrow, and my years with groaning. My strength is gone because of my sins, and my bones are weak.

My enemies mocked me, especially my neighbors. My friends were afraid of me. Those who saw me ran away.

I'm forgotten like a dead person no one remembers. I'm like a broken pot.
