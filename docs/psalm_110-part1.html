Psalm 110

The Lord said to my Lord, "Sit at my right side, until I put your enemies under your feet."

The Lord will send your power from Zion. You will rule among your enemies.

Your people will gladly follow you on the day of your power. They will be beautiful and holy like the morning light. You are fresh and young like the dew.

The Lord has sworn and will not change his mind: you are a priest forever, like Melchizedek.

The Lord at your right hand will strike down kings when he is angry.

He will judge the nations. He will fill the places with dead bodies. He will wound the leaders of many countries.

He will drink from a brook along the path. Then he will lift up his head.
