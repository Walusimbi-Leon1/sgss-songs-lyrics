Psalm 119 — Part 3

Parts: P1 P2 P3 P4 P5 P6 P7 P8 P9 P10

look, I have longed after your instructions: quicken me in your goodness.

Let your kindness come also to me, oh Lord, even your rescue,

according to your word.

So will I have with which to answer him that reproaches me: for I trust in your

word.

Take not the word of truth utterly out of my mouth;

for I have hoped in your judgments.

So will I keep your law continually always and ever.

I will walk at liberty: for I seek your instructions.

I will speak of your promises also before kings, and will not be ashamed.

I will delight myself in your commandments, which I have loved.

My hands also will I lifted up to your commandments, which I have loved;

and I will meditate in your statutes.

Remember the word to your servant, upon which you have caused me to hope.

This is my comfort in my affliction: for your word has quickened me.

The proud have had me greatly in derision: yet have I not declined from your law.

I remembered your judgments of old, oh Lord; and have comforted myself.

Horror has taken hold upon me because of the wicked that forsake your law.

Your rules have been my songs in the house of my pilgrimage.

I have remembered your name, oh Lord, in the night, and have kept your law.

This I had, because I kept your instructions.

You are my portion, oh Lord: I have said that I would keep your words.
