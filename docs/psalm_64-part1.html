Psalm 64

God, hear me when I pray. Keep me safe from my enemies who scare me.

Hide me from the secret plans of the wicked. Protect me from those who rebel and do wrong.

They sharpen their tongues like swords. They bend their bows to shoot arrows—their hurtful words.

They shoot in secret at innocent people. They suddenly attack them and have no fear.

They encourage each other in evil plans. They talk about setting traps in secret. They say, "Who will see us?"

They look for ways to do wrong. They search carefully. Each one's thoughts and heart are hard to know.

But God will shoot them with an arrow. Suddenly they will be hurt.

Their own words will hurt them. Everyone who sees them will run away.

Everyone will be afraid and tell what God has done. They will think carefully about his actions.

Good people will be happy in the Lord and trust him. All with honest hearts will praise him.
