Psalm 143

Lord, hear my prayer. Listen to my requests. Answer me because you are faithful and always do what is right.

Lord, please don't judge me, your servant. No one living can be seen as right in your eyes.

My enemy has chased me and hurt me deep inside. He has knocked my life to the ground. He has left me living in darkness, like those who died long ago.

So my spirit is overwhelmed inside me. My heart feels empty and alone.

I remember the days long ago. I think about all the things you have done. I ponder the work of your hands.

I reach my hands out to you. My soul is thirsty for you, like dry ground thirsty for water. Selah.

Lord, hear me soon. My spirit is giving out
