Psalm 61

God, listen to my cry and pay attention to my prayer.

From the end of the earth I will cry out to you when my heart is overwhelmed. Lead me to the rock that is higher than me.

You have been a safe place for me, a strong tower protecting me from my enemy.

I will stay in your tent forever. I will trust in the shelter of your wings.

Selah.

God, you have heard my promises to you. You have given me the inheritance of those who respect your name.

You will give the king a long life, and his years will last through many generations.

He will stay with God forever. God, please show mercy and truth to keep him safe.

So I will sing praises to you forever, so I can keep my promises to you every day.
