Psalm 139 — Part 1

Parts: P1 P2

Lord, you have searched me and know me inside out.

You know when I sit down and when I stand up. You know my thoughts even from far away.

You are around me when I walk and when I lie down. You know all my ways.

There isn't a word on my tongue that you don't know completely, Lord.

You have surrounded me from behind and in front. You have placed your hand on me.

This knowledge is too amazing for me. It is too high; I cannot reach it.

Where can I go to get away from your Spirit? Where can I run from you?

If I go up to heaven, you are there. If I make my bed in the grave, see, you are there.

If I fly away with the wings of the dawn and live at the farthest end of the sea.

Even there your hand will lead me, and your right hand will hold me.

If I say, "Surely the darkness will cover me," even the night will be light around me.

Yes, darkness doesn't hide from you. The night is as bright as day to you. Dark and light are the same to you.
