Psalm 96

Sing a new song to the Lord! All you on earth, sing to him.

Sing to the Lord and praise his name. Tell about his saving love every day.

Share his glory with the nations. Tell all people about his wonderful works.

The Lord is great and worth much praise. We should honor him above all other gods.

The gods of the nations are just fake idols. But the Lord made the heavens.

Honor and majesty surround him. His holy place is full of strength and beauty.

You families of the earth, give the Lord glory and strength.

Give the Lord the praise his name deserves. Bring a gift and come into his courts.

Worship the Lord in his beautiful holiness. All the earth, stand in awe of him.

Tell the nations that the Lord is king. The world is set firm and won't be shaken. He will judge the people with fairness.

Let the skies be happy and the earth be glad. Let the sea roar and everything in it.

Let the fields be joyful and everything in them. Then all the trees in the forest will rejoice

before the Lord, because he comes to judge the earth. He will judge the world with what is right, and the people with his truth.
