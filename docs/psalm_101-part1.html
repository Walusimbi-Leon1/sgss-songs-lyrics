Psalm 101

I will sing about your kindness and justice. I will sing to you, O Lord.

I will live wisely and do what is right. Lord, when will you come to me? I will walk in my home with a true heart.

I will not look at anything evil. I hate what those who turn away do. It will not stick to me.

A dishonest heart will stay away from me. I will not be friends with anyone evil.

Anyone who secretly talks bad about their neighbor, I will get rid of them. I will not tolerate anyone who is arrogant and proud.

I will watch over the loyal people in the land, so they can live with me. Those who live rightly will serve me.

No one who is deceitful will live in my home. No one who lies will stay near me.

I will quickly remove all evil people from the land, so I can get rid of all who do wrong from the Lord's city.
