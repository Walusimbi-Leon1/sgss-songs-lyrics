Psalm 20

May the Lord hear you when you're in trouble. May the name of the God of Jacob protect you.

May he send you help from his holy place, and give you strength from Zion.

May he remember all the gifts you bring, and accept your burned offerings.

May he give you what your heart desires, and make all your plans succeed.

We will celebrate because you saved us, and we will raise our flags in the name of our God. May the Lord answer all your prayers.

Now I know that the Lord saves his chosen king. He hears him from his holy heaven with the power of his right hand.

Some trust in chariots and some in horses, but we trust in the name of the Lord our God.

They have fallen down, but we have gotten up and stand tall.

Save us, Lord! Let the king hear us when we cry out.
