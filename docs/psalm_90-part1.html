Psalm 90

Lord, you have been our home for all generations.

Before the mountains were made, before you formed the earth and the world, you were God. You have always been God, from forever to forever.

You send people to destruction, and say, "Come back, people."

To you, a thousand years is like just yesterday, like a few hours in the night.

You carry them away like a flood; they are like a dream. In the morning they are like grass that grows up.

In the morning the grass sprouts and grows; by evening it is cut down and dies.

We are worn out by your anger, and troubled by your rage.

You have put our sins in front of you, our hidden sins in the light of your face.

All our days pass away under your anger; we spend our years like a story told.

We live about seventy years, or maybe eighty if we are strong. But even then, our life is hard work and sadness. It is soon over, and we pass away.

Who really knows how strong your anger is? As great as the respect we owe you, so great is your anger.

Teach us to count our days, so we can become wise in our hearts.

Come back, Lord, how long will you wait? Please change your mind and care for us, your servants.

Show us your love early, so we can be happy all our days.

Make us as glad as the days you hurt us, and as many years as we saw bad things.

Show your work to us, your servants, and show your greatness to our children.

Let the goodness of the Lord our God be on us. Please make the work of our hands succeed; yes, make our work succeed.
