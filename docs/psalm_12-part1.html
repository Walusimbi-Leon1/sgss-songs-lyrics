Psalm 12

Lord, help! The people who love you are disappearing. The faithful ones are gone from among the people.

Everyone speaks nonsense to their neighbor. They flatter with their lips and are two-faced in their hearts.

The Lord will silence all who flatter and those who speak with pride.

They say, "We will win by our words. Our lips are our own. Who is the Lord over us?"

Because the poor are oppressed and the needy groan, I will now rise up, says the Lord. I will keep him safe from the one who threatens him.

The Lord's words are pure, like silver melted in a clay furnace and refined seven times.

Lord, you will guard them. You will keep them safe from these people forever.

The wicked roam all around, when the worst people are lifted up.
