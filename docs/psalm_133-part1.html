Psalm 133

Look, how good and pleasant it is when brothers live together as one!

It's like fine oil poured on the head. It ran down the beard, even Aaron's beard. It went down to the bottom of his clothes.

It's like the dew from Mount Hermon. It's also like the dew that falls on the mountains of Zion. For there the Lord gave the blessing of life forever.
