Psalm 147

Praise the Lord! It's good to sing to our God. It's pleasant, and praise is fitting.

The Lord builds up Jerusalem. He gathers the scattered people of Israel together.

He heals those with broken hearts. He bandages their wounds.

He knows exactly how many stars there are. He calls each one by name.

Our Lord is great and very powerful. His understanding has no limit.

The Lord lifts up the humble. He throws the wicked down to the ground.

Sing to the Lord with thankfulness. Play the harp and praise our God.

He covers the sky with clouds. He sends rain to the earth. He makes grass grow on the mountains.

He gives food to the animals. He feeds the crying baby ravens.

He doesn't enjoy the strength of a horse. He isn't pleased by a man's strong legs.

The Lord is happy with those who respect him. He is pleased with those who hope in his mercy.

Praise the Lord, Jerusalem! Praise your God, Zion!

He has made your gates strong. He has blessed your children inside you.

He brings peace to your borders. He fills you with the best wheat.

He sends his command to the earth. His word moves very fast.

He gives snow like wool. He scatters frost like ashes.

He throws out ice like bits. Who can stand his cold?

He sends his word and melts them. He makes his wind blow, and the waters flow.

He shows his word to Jacob. He shows his laws and judgments to Israel.

He hasn't done this with any other nation. They don't know his judgments. Praise the Lord.
