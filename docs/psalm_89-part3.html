Psalm 89 — Part 3

Parts: P1 P2 P3 P4

I will set his hand also in the sea, and his right hand in the rivers.

He will cry to me, you are my father, my God, and the rock of my rescue.

Also I will make him my firstborn, higher than the kings of the earth.

My mercy will I keep for him for evermore, and my covenant will stand fast with

him.

His seed also will I make to endure always, and his throne as the days of

heaven.

If his children forsake my law, and walk not in my judgments;

If they break my rules, and keep not my commandments;

Then will I visit their wrongdoing with the rod,

and their iniquity with stripes.

Nevertheless my love will I not utterly take from him,

nor suffer my faithfulness to fail.

My covenant will I not break, nor alter the thing that is gone out of my lips.

Once have I sworn by my holiness that I will not lie to David.

His seed will endure always, and his throne as the sun before me.

It will be established always as the moon, and as a faithful witness in heaven.

Selah.
