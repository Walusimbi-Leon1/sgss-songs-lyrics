Psalm 87

The Lord's city is built on the holy mountains.

The Lord loves the gates of Zion more than all the homes of Jacob's people.

People say wonderful things about you, city of God. Selah.

I will mention Rahab and Babylon to those who know me. Look, Philistia, Tyre, and Ethiopia—people were born there.

About Zion it will be said, "This one and that one were born there." The Highest God will make her strong.

When the Lord writes down the list of people, he will count them as born there. Selah.

Both singers and musicians will be there. All my life and joy come from you.
