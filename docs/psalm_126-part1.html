Psalm 126

When the Lord brought Zion's captives back, we were like people dreaming.

We laughed and sang. Then the nations said, "The Lord has done great things for them."

The Lord has done great things for us. We are happy.

Lord, bring our captives back, like streams in the dry south.

Those who plant with tears will harvest with joy.

Anyone who goes out weeping, carrying good seed, will surely come back happy, bringing their harvest.
