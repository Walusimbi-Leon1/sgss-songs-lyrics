Psalm 121

I lift my eyes to the hills. That's where my help comes from.

My help comes from the Lord. He made heaven and earth.

He won't let you stumble. The one who watches over you never dozes off.

Look, the one who watches over Israel never sleeps, not even a wink.

The Lord is your protector. The Lord is like shade on your right side.

The sun won't hurt you during the day. The moon won't hurt you at night.

The Lord will keep you safe from all bad things. He will protect your life.

The Lord will keep you safe when you go out and when you come in. He will do this from now on, and forever.
