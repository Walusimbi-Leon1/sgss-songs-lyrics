Psalm 94 — Part 2

Parts: P1 P2

You give them peace during tough times, until a trap is set for the wicked.

The Lord won't reject his people. He won't abandon those he cares for.

But justice will come back to what is right. All honest people will follow it.

Who will stand up for me against those who do evil? Who will help me against those who do wrong?

If the Lord hadn't been my help, I would have been silent forever.

When I said, "I'm slipping," Lord, your kindness held me up.

When I have many thoughts inside, your comfort makes my heart happy.

Can a throne of evil be friends with you, making trouble into law?

They team up against good people, and say innocent people should die.

But the Lord is my protection. My God is my safe place.

He will bring their own evil down on them. He will cut them off because of their wickedness. Yes, the Lord our God will cut them off.
