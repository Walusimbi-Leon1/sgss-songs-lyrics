Psalm 32

Happy is the one whose sins are forgiven, whose wrongs are wiped away.

Happy is the person the Lord doesn't blame for sin, who has no deceit in their heart.

When I stayed silent, my bones ached from groaning all day.

Day and night you pressed down on me, and I dried up like summer heat. Selah.

I told you my sin and didn't hide my wrong. I said, "I will confess my sins to the Lord"; and you forgave me. Selah.

So everyone who loves you should pray to you when you are near. Even in big floods, the water won't reach them.

You are my shelter; you keep me safe from trouble; you wrap me in songs of rescue. Selah.

I will teach you and show you which path to take. I will guide you with my eye.

Don't be like a horse or donkey that doesn't understand. They need a bit and bridle to control them, or they won't come close to you.

Bad people will face many troubles, but those who trust the Lord will be surrounded by his love.

Be happy in the Lord and celebrate, you who do right. Shout for joy, all you with honest hearts.
