Psalm 102 — Part 1

Parts: P1 P2

Listen to my prayer, Lord, and let my cry come to you.

Don't hide your face from me when I'm in trouble. Pay attention to me. When I call, answer me quickly.

My days disappear like smoke. My bones burn like a fire.

My heart is broken and dry like grass. I forget to eat my food.

My bones cling to my skin because I keep groaning.

I'm like a pelican in the wild. I'm like an owl in the desert.

I stay awake, like a sparrow sitting alone on a roof.

My enemies insult me all day. Those who are angry with me team up against me.

I have eaten ashes like bread. I mixed my drink with tears.

You were angry with me. You lifted me up, then threw me down.

My days fade like a shrinking shadow. I am dry like grass.

But you, Lord, will last forever. People will remember you for all generations.

You will rise up and show mercy to Zion. The time to be good to her has come.

Your followers take joy in her stones and care for her dust.
