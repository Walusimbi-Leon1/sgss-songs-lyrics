Psalm 141

Lord, I call out to you. Hurry to me. Listen to my voice when I call out to you.

May my prayer be like incense before you. May my lifted hands be like the evening sacrifice.

Lord, put a watch over my mouth. Guard my lips.

Don't turn my heart to any evil thing, to do wicked deeds with those who do wrong. And don't let me eat their tasty treats.

Let good people correct me; that is a kindness. Let them rebuke me; it will be like good oil that won't hurt my head. Even when they have troubles, I will still pray.

When their leaders are thrown down in rocky places, they will hear my words, for they are kind.

Our bones are scattered at the edge of the grave, like wood chips when someone cuts and splits wood on the ground.

But my eyes are on you, O God the Lord. I trust in you. Don't leave me helpless.

Keep me from the traps they have set for me, and the snares of those who do evil.

Let the wicked fall into their own nets, while I escape.
