Psalm 53

A fool says deep down, "There is no God." But people are corrupt. They have done terrible wrong. No one does good.

God looked down from heaven on people. He checked to see if anyone understood, if anyone was looking for him.

Everyone has turned away. They have all become filthy. No one does good, not one.

Don't those who do evil have any sense? They gobble up my people like they're eating bread. They never pray to God.

They were scared to death where there was nothing to fear. God scattered the bones of the enemy who attacked you. You made them ashamed, because God rejected them.

If only God would save Israel from Zion! When he brings his people home from captivity, Jacob will be happy and Israel will be glad.
