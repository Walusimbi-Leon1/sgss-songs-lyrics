Psalm 115

Not to us, Lord, not to us, but give glory to your name. Do it because you are merciful and true.

Why should the nations say, "Where is their God now?"

But our God is in heaven. He has done whatever he wanted.

Their idols are silver and gold, made by human hands.

They have mouths, but they can't speak. They have eyes, but they can't see.

They have ears, but they can't hear. They have noses, but they can't smell.

They have hands, but they can't touch. They have feet, but they can't walk. They don't even make a sound from their throat.

The people who make those idols are just like them. And so is anyone who trusts in those idols.

Israel, trust in the Lord. He is their helper and their shield.

Family of Aaron, trust in the Lord. He is their helper and their shield.

You who honor the Lord, trust in him. He is their helper and their shield.

The Lord has remembered us. He will bless us. He will bless the people of Israel. He will bless the family of Aaron.

He will bless those who honor the Lord, both young and old.

The Lord will give you more and more, you and your children.

You are blessed by the Lord, who made heaven and earth.

The heavens belong to the Lord. But he has given the earth to people.

The dead don't praise the Lord. Neither do those who are silent in the grave.

But we will bless the Lord from now on and forever. Praise the Lord!
