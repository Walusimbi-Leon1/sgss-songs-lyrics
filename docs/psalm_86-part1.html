Psalm 86

Lord, please listen to me, because I am poor and in need.

Keep me safe, because I belong to you. My God, save me, your servant, who trusts in you.

Lord, be kind to me, because I call to you every day.

Bring joy to your servant's heart, because I lift my soul to you, Lord.

Lord, you are good and quick to forgive. You show great mercy to everyone who calls to you.

Lord, listen to my prayer. Pay attention to my cries for help.

When I am in trouble, I will call to you, because you will answer me.
