Psalm 125

Those who trust in the Lord are like Mount Zion. They cannot be moved, and they stay forever.

Just as the mountains are all around Jerusalem, the Lord is all around his people, now and forever.

The wicked will not keep their power over the righteous. This is so the righteous don't reach out to do evil.

Lord, please do good to those who are good, and to those who are honest in their hearts.

Those who turn aside to their crooked ways, the Lord will lead them away with those who do evil. But peace will be with Israel.
