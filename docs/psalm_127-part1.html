Psalm 127

Unless the Lord builds the house, the builders work for nothing. Unless the Lord guards the city, the watchman stays awake for nothing.

It's useless for you to get up early and stay up late, eating food with worry. Because the Lord gives sleep to those he loves.

Look, children are a gift from the Lord. The babies you have are his reward.

Kids you have when you're young are like arrows in the hands of a strong warrior.

Happy is the person who has many children. They won't be ashamed, but they will talk with their enemies at the city gate.
