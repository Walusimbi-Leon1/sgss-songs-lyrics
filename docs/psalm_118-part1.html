Psalm 118 — Part 1

Parts: P1 P2

Thank the Lord, because he is good. His love never ends.

Let Israel say, his love never ends.

Let the family of Aaron say, his love never ends.

Let those who respect the Lord say, his love never ends.

I called to the Lord when I was in trouble. He answered me and gave me a safe, open space.

The Lord is with me. I won't be afraid. What can anyone do to me?

The Lord is on my side with those who help me. So I will see my wish come true against those who hate me.

It's better to trust the Lord than to rely on people.

It's better to trust the Lord than to rely on rulers.

All the nations surrounded me. But by the Lord's power I will defeat them.

They surrounded me, yes, they surrounded me. But by the Lord's power I will defeat them.

They swarmed around me like bees. They burned out like a fire of thorns. But by the Lord's power I will defeat them.

You pushed me hard to make me fall. But the Lord helped me.

The Lord is my strength and my song. He has become the one who saves me.

The sound of joy and rescue is in the homes of the good people. The Lord's right hand acts with power.
