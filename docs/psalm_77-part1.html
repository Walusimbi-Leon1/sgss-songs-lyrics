Psalm 77

I called out to God with my voice, calling to him, and he heard me.

When I was in trouble, I looked for the Lord. My pain kept going through the night and didn't stop. I couldn't let myself be comforted.

I thought of God and felt troubled. I grumbled, and my spirit felt crushed. Selah.

You keep my eyes awake. I'm so troubled I can't even talk.

I've thought about the old days, the years long ago.

I remember the song I sang at night. I talk with my own heart, and my spirit searches hard.

Will the Lord reject us forever? Will he never be kind again?

Is his mercy completely gone forever? Does his promise fail for all time?

Has God forgotten to be good? Has he angrily closed off his gentle mercy? Selah.

I said, this is my weakness, but I will remember the times when the Most High God used his power to help.

I will remember what the Lord has done. Yes, I will remember your amazing works from long ago.

I will think about all your work, and speak of what you have done.

Your path, O God, is in the holy place. Who is as great a God as our God?

You are the God who does amazing things. You have shown your strength among the people.

With your strong arm you saved your people, the descendants of Jacob and Joseph. Selah.

The waters saw you, O God, the waters saw you. They were afraid. The deep places were shaken.

The clouds poured out rain. The skies made a sound. Your arrows flew out.

Your thunder sounded in the sky. The lightning lit up the world. The earth trembled and shook.

Your path is in the sea, your road in the huge waters, but your footprints are not seen.

You led your people like a flock of sheep, through Moses and Aaron.
