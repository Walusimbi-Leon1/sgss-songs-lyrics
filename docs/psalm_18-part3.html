For you will save the hurt people; but will bring down high looks.

For you will light my candle: the Lord my God will enlighten my darkness.

For by you I have run through a troop; and by my God have I leaped over a wall.

As for God, his way is perfect: the word of the Lord is tried: he is a buckler to all

those that trust in him.

For who is God save the Lord? or who is a rock save our God?

It is God that girds me with power, and makes my way perfect.

He makes my feet like hinds’ feet, and setts me upon my high places.

He teaches my hands to war, so that a bow of steel is broken by mine arms.

You have also given me the shield of your rescue: and your right hand has held me

up, and your gentleness has made me great.

You have enlarged my steps under me, that my feet did not slip.

I have pursued mine enemies, and overtaken them: neither did I turn again till they

were consumed.

I have wounded them that they were not able to rise: they are fallen under my

feet.

For you have girded me with power to the battle: you have subdued under me those

that rose up against me.
