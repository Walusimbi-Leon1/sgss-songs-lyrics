Psalm 122

I was happy when they said to me, "Let's go to the Lord's house."

Our feet will stand inside your gates, Jerusalem.

Jerusalem is built like a city that is joined together.

There the tribes go up, the tribes of the Lord, to the meeting place of Israel, to say thank you to the Lord.

That is because there are seats for judging, the seats of David's family.

Pray for peace in Jerusalem: those who love you will do well.

May there be peace inside your walls, and good things inside your palaces.

For my brothers and friends, I will say, "May you have peace."

Because of the Lord our God's house, I will want the best for you.
