Psalm 85

Lord, you have been kind to your land: you have brought back the people of Jacob from captivity.

You have forgiven your people's wrongdoing, you have covered over all their sins.

Selah.

You have taken away all your anger: you have turned away from being fiercely angry.

Bring us back, God our Savior, and stop being angry with us.

Will you stay angry with us forever? Will you keep your anger going for all generations?

Won't you give us new life again, so your people can be happy in you?

Show us your kindness, Lord, and give us your saving help.

I will listen to what God the Lord says, because he speaks peace to his people and to those who follow him. But they must not go back to foolish ways.

Surely his saving help is near those who honor him, so that glory can live in our land.

Kindness and truth have come together; doing right and peace have kissed each other.

Truth will grow up from the earth; and doing right will look down from heaven.

Yes, the Lord will give good things; and our land will produce its harvest.

Doing right will go before him; and will set us on the path of his footsteps.
