Psalm 8

Lord, our Lord, your name is so wonderful all over the earth! You have put your glory above the heavens.

From the mouths of babies and little children, you have given strength. You did this because of your enemies. You wanted to silence the enemy and the one who wants revenge.

When I look at your skies, the work of your hands, and the moon and stars you put there.

What is a person, that you think of him? What is a human, that you come to him?

You made him just a little lower than the angels. You gave him glory and honor.

You let him rule over everything you made. You put all things under his control.

All sheep and cattle, and also the wild animals.

The birds in the sky, the fish in the sea, and everything that moves through the ocean.

Lord, our Lord, your name is so wonderful all over the earth!
