Psalm 114

When Israel left Egypt, Jacob's family got away from people who spoke a strange language.

Judah was the Lord's special place, and Israel was the land he ruled.

The sea saw this and ran away. The Jordan River was pushed back.

The mountains jumped like big sheep, and the small hills like little lambs.

What happened to you, sea, that you ran away? Jordan, why were you pushed back?

Mountains, why did you jump like big sheep? Small hills, why did you leap like little lambs?

Earth, shake with fear when the Lord is near, when the God of Jacob is here.

He turned the rock into a pool of water, and the hard stone into a spring.
