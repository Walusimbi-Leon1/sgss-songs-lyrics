Psalm 45

My heart is pouring out a good message. I speak about the things I have made about the king. My tongue is like a pen of a ready writer.

You are more beautiful than all people. Grace pours from your lips. That is why God has blessed you forever.

Put your sword on your thigh, you mighty one, with your glory and majesty.

Ride out in your majesty and succeed. Do this because you are true, gentle, and right. Your right hand will show you powerful things.

Your arrows are sharp in the hearts of the king's enemies. Because of this, the people fall before you
