Psalm 119 — Part 4

Parts: P1 P2 P3 P4 P5 P6 P7 P8 P9 P10

I intreated your favour with my whole heart: be kind to me according to your

word.

I thought on my ways, and turned my feet to your promises.

I made haste, and delayed not to keep your commandments.

The bands of the wicked have robbed me: but I have not forgotten your law.

At midnight I will rise to give thanks to you because of your good

judgments.

I am a companion of all them that fear you, and of them that keep your instructions.

The earth, oh Lord, is full of your mercy: teach me your rules.

You have dealt well with your servant, oh Lord, according to your word.

Teach me good judgment and knowledge: for I have believed your commandments.

Before I was hurt I went astray: but now have I kept your word.

You are good, and do good; teach me your rules.

The proud have forged a lie against me: but I will keep your instructions with my whole

heart.

Their heart is as fat as grease; but I delight in your law.

It is good for me that I have been hurt; that I might learn your rules.

The law of your mouth is better to me than thousands of gold and silver.

Your hands have made me and fashioned me: give me understanding,

that I may learn your commandments.

They that fear you will be glad when they see me;

because I have hoped in your word.
