Psalm 35

Lord, take my side against those who fight me. Fight those who fight me.

Grab your shield and come help me.

Pull out your spear too, and block the road for those who hurt me. Tell me, "I am your salvation."

Make those who want to harm me feel ashamed and confused. Make those who plan to hurt me turn back and be mixed up.

Let them be like worthless husks blown by the wind. Let the Lord's angel chase them away.

Make their path dark and slippery. Let the Lord's angel hunt them down.

For no reason, they hid a trap in a pit for me. For no reason, they dug it to catch me.

Let ruin come to him suddenly. Let the trap he hid catch him instead. Let him fall into that same ruin.

I will be happy in the Lord. I will celebrate his rescue.

Every part of me will say, "Lord, who is like you? You save the weak from the strong. You rescue the poor and needy from those who ruin them."
