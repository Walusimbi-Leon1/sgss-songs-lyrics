Psalm 7

Lord my God, I trust in you. Save me from everyone who is hurting me, and rescue me.

Otherwise, he will tear me apart like a lion, ripping me to pieces, and no one will rescue me.

Lord my God, if I have done this wrong thing; if I have sinned with my hands;

if I have paid back evil to someone who was friendly to me; (yes, I have even helped my enemy who had no reason to hate me:)

then let my enemy chase me and take my life; let him crush me on the ground and throw my honor in the dirt. Selah.

Get up, Lord, be angry at my enemies, rise up because they rage against me. Wake up and bring the fair judgment you commanded.

Then all the people will gather around you. For their sake, rise up high again.
