Song of Solomon 6

Where did your love go, you who are the most beautiful woman? Where did he wander off to? We want to look for him with you.

My love went down to his garden, to the spice beds, to eat in the gardens and pick lilies.

I belong to my love, and he belongs to me. He is eating among the lilies.

You are beautiful, my love, like Tirzah, lovely like Jerusalem, and strong like an army with flags.

Look away from me, your eyes overwhelm me. Your hair flows like a flock of goats coming down from Gilead.

Your teeth are like a flock of sheep just washed, each one having twins, and not one is missing.

Your cheeks behind your hair are like a slice of pomegranate.

There are sixty queens, eighty wives, and too many young women to count.

My dove, my pure one, is the only one. She is her mother's only daughter, the favorite of the one who bore her. The young women saw her and called her blessed. The queens and wives praised her too.

Who is this woman shining like the dawn, pretty as the moon, bright as the sun, and strong like an army with flags?

I went down to the nut garden to see the valley fruits, and to check if the vines were growing and the pomegranates were blooming.

Before I knew it, my heart swept me away like a fast chariot.

Come back, come back, Shulamite! Come back so we can look at you. What do we see in the Shulamite? It's like two armies standing together.
