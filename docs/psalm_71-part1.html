Psalm 71 — Part 1

Parts: P1 P2

In you, oh Lord, do I put my trust: let me never be put to confusion.

Deliver me in your goodness, and cause me to escape: incline your ear to me,

and save me.

Be you my strong habitation, whereunto I may continually resort: you have given

commandment to save me; for you are my rock and my fortress.

Deliver me, oh my God, out of the hand of the wicked,

out of the hand of the unrighteous and cruel man.

For you are my hope, oh Lord God: you are my trust from my youth.

By you have I been held up from the womb: you are he that took me out of my mother’s

bowels: my praise shall be continually of you.

I am as a wonder to many; but you are my strong safe place.

Let my mouth be filled with your thank and with your honour all the day.

Cast me not off in the time of old age; forsake me not when my power fails.

For mine enemies speak against me; and they that lay wait for my soul take counsel

together,

say, God has forsaken him: persecute and take him;

for there is none to deliver him.

oh God, be not far from me: oh my God, make haste for my help.

Let them be confounded and consumed that are adversaries to my soul;

let them be covered with reproach and dishonour that seek my hurt.

But I will hope continually, and will yet thank you more and more.
