Psalm 25

Lord, I lift my soul to you.

My God, I trust you. Don't let me be ashamed. Don't let my enemies defeat me.

And don't let anyone who hopes in you be ashamed. Let those who do wrong for no reason be the ones who are ashamed.

Lord, show me your ways. Teach me your paths.

Guide me in your truth and teach me, because you are the God who saves me. I wait for you all day long.

Lord, remember your mercy and your love, because they have been there from long ago.

Don't remember the sins of my youth or the wrong things I've done. Because you are merciful, remember me for your goodness, Lord.

The Lord is good and fair. So he will teach those who do wrong the right way.

He will guide the humble in doing what is right. He will teach them his way.

Everything the Lord does is full of mercy and truth for those who keep his promises and obey his words.

Lord, for the sake of your name, forgive my sin, because it is great.

Who is the person who fears the Lord? He will teach them the way they should choose.

They will live in peace, and their children will inherit the earth.

The Lord shares his friendship with those who respect him, and he shows them his promises.

I always look to the Lord, because he will pull my feet out of the trap.

Turn to me and have mercy on me, because I am lonely and in pain.

My heart is full of troubles. Please bring me out of my distress.

See my suffering and my pain, and forgive all my sins.

Look at my enemies, because there are many, and they hate me cruelly.

Protect my life and save me. Don't let me be ashamed, because I trust you.

Let my honesty and goodness keep me safe, because I wait for you.

God, rescue Israel from all their troubles.
