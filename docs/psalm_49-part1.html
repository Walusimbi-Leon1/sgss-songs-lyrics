Psalm 49

Listen, all you people. Pay attention, everyone in the world:

Whether you are poor or rich, low or high, we're all together.

I'm going to speak with wisdom. The thoughts of my heart will be full of understanding.

I will listen closely to a story with a lesson. I will play my harp and explain a mystery.

Why should I be scared during bad times, when the wrong things I've done follow me around?

Some people trust in their money and brag about how much they have.

But none of them can ever save their brother or pay God to rescue him.

Because saving a soul is too costly, and it can't be done forever.

You can't pay so that someone lives forever and never decays.

We see that wise people die, and so do foolish and stupid people. They all leave their money to others.

In their hearts they think their homes will last forever, and their places will stay for generations. They name their land after themselves.

But a person with honor doesn't stay that way. He is like an animal that dies.

This way of living is foolish, but their kids agree with their words. Selah.

They are buried like sheep. Death feeds on them. But the good people will have power over them in the morning. Their beauty fades in the grave, away from the living.

But God will save my soul from the grave's power, because he will take me in. Selah.

Don't be afraid when someone becomes rich and their house gets more famous.

Because when he dies, he takes nothing with him. His fame doesn't follow him.

While he lived, he felt good about himself. People praise you when you do well for yourself.

He will join his ancestors who died. They will never see light again.

A person who has honor but doesn't understand is like an animal that dies.
