Psalm 41

Happy is the person who cares for the poor. The Lord will rescue him when trouble comes.

The Lord will protect him and keep him alive. He will be blessed here on earth. You won't hand him over to his enemies.

The Lord will give him strength when he is sick in bed. You will care for him and make his bed in his sickness.

I said, "Lord, have mercy on me. Heal my soul, because I have sinned against you."

My enemies say bad things about me. They ask, "When will he die and be forgotten?"

If he comes to see me, he speaks nonsense. His heart fills with sin. When he goes out, he spreads it around.

Everyone who hates me whispers together against me. They plan to hurt me.
