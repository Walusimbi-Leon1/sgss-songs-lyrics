Psalm 131

Lord, I'm not proud, and I don't act like I'm better than others. I don't busy myself with big things or matters too hard for me.

I have settled down and become quiet, like a little child who has stopped nursing from his mother. My heart is like that calm child.

Israel, hope in the Lord from now on and forever.
