Psalm 6

Lord, don't scold me when you're angry, and don't punish me when you're really upset.

Lord, have mercy on me because I am weak. Lord, heal me, my bones hurt badly.

I am also deeply troubled. But Lord, how long will this last?

Lord, come and save me. Rescue me because you are merciful.

If I die, I can't remember you. In the grave, who can thank you?

I'm tired from groaning. All night I cry, and my bed is soaked with tears.

My eyes are worn out from sadness. They are tired because of all my enemies.

Go away, all you who do evil, because the Lord has heard me crying.

The Lord has heard my plea. The Lord will accept my prayer.

May all my enemies be embarrassed and troubled. May they turn back and be ashamed quickly.
