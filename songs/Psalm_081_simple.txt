Psalm 81

1 Sing loudly to God, our strength. Make a happy noise to the God of Jacob.

2 Take a song, and bring the tambourine, the nice harp, and the stringed instrument.

3 Blow the trumpet at the new moon, at the set time, on our special feast day.

4 This was a rule for Israel, and a law from the God of Jacob.

5 He set this up for Joseph as a reminder when he left Egypt. There I heard a language I didn't understand.

6 I took the load off his shoulder. His hands were freed from the pots.

7 You called me when you were in trouble, and I rescued you. I answered you in the hidden thunder. I tested you at the waters of Meribah. Selah.

8 Listen, my people, and I will tell you this: Israel, if you will listen to me;

9 Don't let any other god be among you. Don't bow down to any foreign god.

10 I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of Egypt. Open your mouth wide, and I will fill it.

11 But my people would not listen to my voice. Israel didn't want me.

12 So I let them follow their own desires. They went by their own advice.

13 I wish my people had listened to me. I wish Israel had walked in my ways!

14 I would have quickly beaten their enemies. I would have turned to fight their foes.

15 Those who hate the Lord would have given in to him. Their good times would have lasted forever.

16 He would have given them the best wheat. And I would have satisfied you with honey from the rock.
