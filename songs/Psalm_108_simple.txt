Psalm 108

1 God, my heart is set. I will sing and praise you with all of me.
2 Wake up, my harp and lyre. I will wake up early too.
3 I will praise you, Lord, among the people. I will sing to you among the nations.
4 Your love is great above the heavens. Your truth reaches to the clouds.
5 God, be lifted high above the heavens. Let your glory be over all the earth.
6 Rescue those you love. Save us with your power, and answer me.
7 God has spoken from his holy place. I will be glad. I will divide Shechem and measure the valley of Succoth.
8 Gilead is mine. Manasseh is mine. Ephraim protects my head. Judah
