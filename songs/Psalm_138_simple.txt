Psalm 138

1 I will praise you with all my heart. Before other gods, I will sing songs of praise to you.

2 I will worship toward your holy temple. I will praise your name because you are loving and true. You have made your word more important than anything, even your own name.

3 When I cried out to you, you answered me. You gave me strength in my soul.

4 All the kings of the earth will praise you, Lord, when they hear what you say.

5 Yes, they will sing as they follow the Lord's ways, because the Lord's glory is so great.

6 Even though the Lord is high above all, he cares for the humble. But he keeps his distance from the proud.

7 Even when I walk through hard times, you will give me life. You will reach out your hand to stop my enemies' anger, and your power will save me.

8 The Lord will finish what he started in my life. Your love, Lord, lasts forever. Please don't abandon what you have made with your own hands.
