Psalm 4

1 Listen to me when I call, my God who makes me right: you helped me when I was in trouble; be kind to me and hear my prayer.
2 Hey people, how long will you keep shaming my honor? How long will you love empty things and chase after lies? Selah.
3 But know that the Lord has made those who are faithful special to him: the Lord will listen when I call to him.
4 Respect the Lord and don't sin: talk with your own heart while on your bed, and be quiet. Selah.
5 Give the right sacrifices and trust the Lord.
6 Many people say, "Who will show us anything good?" Lord, look at us with your kind face.
7 You have made my heart happier than when their grain and wine grew.
8 I will lie down in peace and sleep, because you, Lord, are the only one who keeps me safe.
