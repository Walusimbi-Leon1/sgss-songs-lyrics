Psalm 4

1 My God, you make me right. Hear me when I call. You helped me when I was in trouble. Be kind to me and listen to my prayer.
2 Hey people, how long will you bring shame to my good name? How long will you love empty things and chase after lies? Selah.
3 But know this: the Lord has chosen the good person for himself. The Lord will hear me when I call to him.
4 Respect the Lord and don't sin. When you're in bed, talk with your own heart and be quiet. Selah.
5 Bring the right sacrifices and trust in the Lord.
6 Many people say, "Who will show us something good?" Lord, please shine your friendly face on us.
7 You have made my heart glad, more than when their grain and wine grew abundant.
8 I will lie down in peace and sleep, because you, Lord, are the only one who keeps me safe.
