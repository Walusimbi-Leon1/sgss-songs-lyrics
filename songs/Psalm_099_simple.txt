Psalm 99

1 The Lord is king. Let the people tremble. He sits between the cherubim. Let the earth shake.
2 The Lord is great in Zion. He is high above all people.
3 Let them praise your great and awesome name, because it is holy.
4 The King is strong and loves justice. You make things fair. You judge rightly and do what is right for the people of Jacob.
5 Praise the Lord our God, and worship at his feet, because he is holy.
6 Moses and Aaron were his priests. Samuel was among those who call on his name. They called to the Lord, and he answered them.
7 He spoke to them from the cloudy pillar. They kept his teachings and the rules he gave them.
8 You answered them, Lord our God. You forgave them, even though you punished them for the bad things they did.
9 Praise the Lord our God, and worship on his holy hill, because the Lord our God is holy.
