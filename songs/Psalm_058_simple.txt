Psalm 58

1 Do you really speak what is right, you community? Do you judge fairly, you people?
2 Yes, in your heart you do evil. You measure out violence with your hands on earth.
3 The wicked turn away from birth. They go astray as soon as they are born, telling lies.
4 Their poison is like the poison of a snake. They are like a deaf snake that blocks its ear.
5 They do not listen to the voice of snake charmers, no matter how wisely they charm.
6 Break their teeth in their mouth, God. Smash the big teeth of the young lions, Lord.
7 Let them melt away like flowing water. When someone bends a bow to shoot arrows, let them be cut to pieces.
8 Like a snail that melts, let each of them disappear. Like a baby born too early, so they never see the sun.
9 Before your pots feel the thorns, he will take them away in a whirlwind, both living and in his anger.
10 The good person will rejoice when he sees the payback. He will wash his feet in the blood of the wicked.
11 So that someone will say, Truly there is a reward for those who do right: truly he is a God who judges on earth.
