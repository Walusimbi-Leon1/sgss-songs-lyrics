Psalm 24

1 The whole earth belongs to the Lord, and everything in it. The world and every person living there.
2 He set it on the oceans and made it steady on the waves.
3 Who can go up to the Lord's hill? Who can stand in his holy place?
4 It's the one with clean hands and a pure heart. The one who hasn't chased after worthless things or told lies.
5 That person will receive a blessing from the Lord, and be made right by the God who saves them.
6 These are the people who seek him, who look for you, Jacob. Selah.
7 Lift up your heads, you gates! Rise up, you eternal doors! The glorious King is coming in.
8 Who is this glorious King? The Lord is strong and mighty, the Lord is powerful in battle.
9 Lift up your heads, you gates! Rise up, you eternal doors! The glorious King is coming in.
10 Who is this glorious King? The Lord of heaven's armies, he is the glorious King. Selah.
