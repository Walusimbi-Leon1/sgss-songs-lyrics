Psalm 17

1 Lord, hear what is right. Pay attention to my cry for help. Listen to my prayer. It doesn't come from fake lips.

2 Let my case be decided by you. May your eyes see what is fair.

3 You have tested my heart. You checked on me at night. You tried me and found nothing wrong. I'm determined not to say anything bad.

4 Because of what people do, I have stayed away from the paths of those who destroy, obeying what you said.

5 Keep me steady on your path, so my feet don't slip.

6 I called to you, God, because you listen. Please turn your ear to me and hear what I say.

7 Show your wonderful love, O God. You save with your strong hand those who trust you from people who rise against them. Please do that for me.

8 Keep me safe like the pupil of your eye. Hide me under your wing.

9 Protect me from the wicked who oppress me, from my deadly enemies who circle around me.

10 They are stuffed with their own comfort. They talk big and proud.

11 They now circle around us as we walk. They keep their eyes fixed on the ground, watching us.

12 They are like a lion eager for its prey, like a young lion hiding in secret spots.

13 Lord, rise up. Defeat him. Knock him down. Rescue me from the wicked, who is your sword.

14 Protect me from those men who are your tools, O Lord, from people of this world. They get their good things in this life. You fill their stomachs with hidden blessings. They have many children and leave their money to their little ones.

15 But me, I will see your face because I am righteous. I'll be happy when I wake up, seeing you as you are.
