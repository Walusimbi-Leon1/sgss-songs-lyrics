Psalm 42

1 Like a deer that pants for streams of water, my soul longs for you, God.
2 My soul is thirsty for God, the living God. When can I go and be with him?
3 I cry so much that my food all day and night is tears. My enemies keep saying to me, "Where is your God?"
4 When I think about all this, I feel like I'm falling apart inside. I used to go with the crowd to God's house, singing and praising with everyone at the festival.
5 Why are you so sad, my soul? Why are you so upset inside me? Put your hope in God, because I will still praise him for his help and his smile.
6 My God, my soul feels sad deep inside me. So I will remember you even here in the land of Jordan, and from Hermon and the hill of Mizar.
7 One deep water calls to another at the roar of your waterfalls. All your waves and breakers have crashed over me.
8 But the Lord will show his love during the day, and at night his song will be with me, as I pray to the God who gives me life.
9 I will say to God, my rock, "Why have you forgotten me? Why do I walk around sad because my enemy is crushing me?"
10 My enemies insult me, and it hurts like a sword stabbing my bones. They say to me every day, "Where is your God?"
11 Why are you so sad, my soul? Why are you so upset inside me? Put your hope in God, because I will still praise him—he is my joy and my God.
