Psalm 44

1 God, we've heard with our own ears what our fathers told us. They told us about the amazing things you did in their days, long ago.
2 You drove out the nations with your own hand and planted our people there. You hurt those other people and threw them out.
3 They didn't take the land with their own sword. Their own strength didn't save them. It was your hand, your strength, and your smiling face, because you were pleased with them.
4 You are my King, God. Bring victory for Jacob.
5 With you we will knock down our enemies. By your name we will stomp on those who rise up against us.
6 I won't trust in my bow. My sword won't save me.
7 But you have saved us from our enemies. You made those who hated us look foolish.
8 We boast in God all day long, and we praise your name forever. Selah.
9 But now you have pushed us away and made us ashamed. You don't go out with our armies anymore.
10 You make us run away from the enemy. Those who hate us take our stuff for themselves.
