Psalm 29

1 You strong ones, give the Lord glory and strength.
2 Give the Lord the glory that belongs to his name. Worship the Lord in the beauty of his holiness.
3 The Lord's voice is over the waters. The God of glory thunders. The Lord is above the many waters.
4 The Lord's voice is powerful. His voice is full of greatness.
5 The Lord's voice breaks the cedars. Yes, the Lord breaks the cedars of Lebanon.
6 He makes them skip like a calf. Lebanon and Sirion leap like a young ox.
7 The Lord's voice splits the flames of fire.
8 The Lord's voice shakes the desert. The Lord shakes the desert of Kadesh.
9 The Lord's voice makes the deer give birth. It uncovers the forests. And in his temple, everyone talks about his glory.
10 The Lord sits above the flood. Yes, the Lord sits as King forever.
11 The Lord will give strength to his people. The Lord will bless his people with peace.
