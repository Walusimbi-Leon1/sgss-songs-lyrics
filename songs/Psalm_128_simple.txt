Psalm 128

1 Happy is everyone who respects the Lord and follows his ways.
2 You will enjoy the food you worked for. You will be happy, and life will go well for you.
3 Your wife will be like a healthy vine by your house, full of fruit. Your children will be like little olive trees around your table.
4 Look, that's how a person who respects the Lord will be happy.
5 The Lord will bless you from Zion. You will see good times in Jerusalem every day of your life.
6 Yes, you will see your grandkids, and peace in Israel.
