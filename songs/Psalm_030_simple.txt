Psalm 30

1 I will praise you, Lord, because you lifted me up and didn't let my enemies celebrate over me.
2 Lord my God, I cried out to you, and you healed me.
3 Lord, you pulled my soul from the grave. You kept me alive so I wouldn't go down into the pit.
4 Sing to the Lord, you his people, and give thanks when you remember how holy he is.
5 His anger lasts only a moment. But when he is pleased with us, there is life. We may cry through the night, but joy comes in the morning.
6 When things were going well, I said, "I will never be shaken."
7 Lord, because you were pleased with me, you made me stand strong like a mountain. But then you turned away, and I became upset.
8 I cried to you, Lord, and I begged you for help.
9 What good comes from my dying? If I go to the grave, will the dust praise you? Will it speak your truth?
10 Listen, Lord, and be kind to me. Lord, be my helper.
11 You changed my sadness into dancing. You took away my sad clothes and dressed me with happiness.
12 So that my heart can sing praise to you and not stay quiet. Lord my God, I will thank you forever.
