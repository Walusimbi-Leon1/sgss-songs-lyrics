Psalm 55

1 Listen to my prayer, God, and don't hide from my cry for help.
2 Pay attention to me and listen. I am sad and complaining, and I cry out loud.
3 It's because of my enemy's voice and the wicked hurting me: they accuse me of evil, and in anger they hate me.
4 My heart aches deep inside, and I'm terrified, feeling like death is near.
5 I am full of fear and shaking, and horror has taken over me.
6 I said, "If only I had wings like a dove! Then I could fly away and find rest."
7 I would go far away and live in the wilderness. Selah
8 I would hurry to escape the raging storm.
9 Lord, destroy them and confuse their talk, because I have seen violence and fighting in the city.
10 They walk around its walls day and night; trouble and sadness are inside it.
11 Evil is in the middle of it; lies and trickery never leave its streets.
12 It wasn't an enemy who insulted me—I could have handled that. It wasn't someone who hated me who attacked me; I would have hidden from him.
13 But it was you, someone like me, my friend and guide.
