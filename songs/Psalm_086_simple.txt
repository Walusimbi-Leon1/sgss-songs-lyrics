Psalm 86

1 Lord, please listen to me, because I am poor and in need.
2 Keep me safe, because I belong to you. My God, save me, your servant, who trusts in you.
3 Lord, be kind to me, because I call to you every day.
4 Bring joy to your servant's heart, because I lift my soul to you, Lord.
5 Lord, you are good and quick to forgive. You show great mercy to everyone who calls to you.
6 Lord, listen to my prayer. Pay attention to my cries for help.
7 When I am in trouble, I will call to you, because you will answer me.
