Psalm 122

1 I was happy when they said to me, "Let's go to the Lord's house."
2 Our feet will stand inside your gates, Jerusalem.
3 Jerusalem is built like a city that is joined together.
4 There the tribes go up, the tribes of the Lord, to the meeting place of Israel, to say thank you to the Lord.
5 That is because there are seats for judging, the seats of David's family.
6 Pray for peace in Jerusalem: those who love you will do well.
7 May there be peace inside your walls, and good things inside your palaces.
8 For my brothers and friends, I will say, "May you have peace."
9 Because of the Lord our God's house, I will want the best for you.
