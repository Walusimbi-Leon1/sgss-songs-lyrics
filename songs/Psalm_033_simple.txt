Psalm 33

1 Be glad in the Lord, you good people, because it's right for honest folks to praise him.
2 Praise the Lord with a harp. Sing to him with a ten-string instrument.
3 Sing a new song to him. Play it well and make it loud.
4 The Lord's word is right, and everything he does is true.
5 He loves what is right and fair. The earth is full of the Lord's goodness.
6 The Lord made the heavens by his word. He made all the stars by his breath.
7 He gathers the sea waters into a pile. He keeps the deep waters in storage.
8 Everyone on earth should respect the Lord. All people should be amazed at him.
9 He just spoke, and it happened. He gave a command, and it stayed there.
10 The Lord makes the plans of the nations useless. He ruins the schemes of the people.
11 But the Lord's plans last forever. His thoughts last for all generations.
12 The nation whose God is the Lord is happy. The people he chose for himself are also happy.
13 The Lord looks down from heaven. He sees all people.
14 From his home, he watches everyone on earth.
15 He made their hearts. He pays attention to everything they do.
16 No king is saved by having a big army. A strong man is not rescued by his own power.
17 A horse is useless for saving you. It can't rescue anyone by its strength.
18 Look, the Lord watches those who respect him, those who hope in his love.
19 He does this to save them from death and keep them alive during famine.
20 We wait for the Lord. He is our help and our shield.
21 Our hearts will be glad in him, because we trust his holy name.
22 Lord, show us your love, because we hope in you.
