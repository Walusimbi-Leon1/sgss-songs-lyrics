Psalm 110

1 The Lord said to my Lord, "Sit at my right side, until I put your enemies under your feet."
2 The Lord will send your power from Zion. You will rule among your enemies.
3 Your people will gladly follow you on the day of your power. They will be beautiful and holy like the morning light. You are fresh and young like the dew.
4 The Lord has sworn and will not change his mind: you are a priest forever, like Melchizedek.
5 The Lord at your right hand will strike down kings when he is angry.
6 He will judge the nations. He will fill the places with dead bodies. He will wound the leaders of many countries.
7 He will drink from a brook along the path. Then he will lift up his head.
