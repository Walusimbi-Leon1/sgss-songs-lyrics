Psalm 45

1 My heart is pouring out a good message. I speak about the things I have made about the king. My tongue is like a pen of a ready writer.
2 You are more beautiful than all people. Grace pours from your lips. That is why God has blessed you forever.
3 Put your sword on your thigh, you mighty one, with your glory and majesty.
4 Ride out in your majesty and succeed. Do this because you are true, gentle, and right. Your right hand will show you powerful things.
5 Your arrows are sharp in the hearts of the king's enemies. Because of this, the people fall before you
