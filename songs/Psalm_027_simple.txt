Psalm 27

1 The Lord is my light and my salvation; who should I fear? The Lord is the strength of my life; who should I be afraid of?
2 When the wicked, my enemies and those who hate me, came at me to harm me, they tripped and fell.
3 Even if an army surrounds me, I will not be afraid. Even if war breaks out against me, I will be sure of this.
4 There is one thing I want from the Lord, and that is what I will seek: to live in the Lord's house every day of my life, to see the beauty of the Lord, and to ask him in his temple.
5 When trouble comes, he will hide me in his tent. He will keep me safe in his shelter and set me on a high rock.
6 Now he will lift my head high above my enemies all around me. So I will bring happy offerings in his house. I will sing, yes, I will sing praises to the Lord.
7 Lord, hear me when I cry out to you. Be kind to me and answer me.
8 When you said, "Look for my face," my heart said to you, "Lord, I will look for you."
9 Don't turn your face away from me. Don't push me away when you are angry. You have been my help. Don't leave me or forget me, God who saves me.
10 Even if my father and mother leave me, the Lord will take care of me.
11 Lord, teach me your way. Lead me on a clear path because of my enemies.
12 Don't hand me over to my enemies. People are telling lies about me, and they want to hurt me badly.
13 I would have given up if I had not believed I would see the Lord's goodness in this life.
14 Wait for the Lord. Be brave, and he will make your heart strong. I say again, wait for the Lord.
