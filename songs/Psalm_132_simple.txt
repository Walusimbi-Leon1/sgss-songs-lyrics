Psalm 132

1 Lord, remember David and all his troubles:
2 He made a promise to the Lord, and vowed to the powerful God of Jacob.
3 I won't go into my house, or go to my bed,
4 I won't let my eyes sleep, or my eyelids rest,
5 until I find a place for the Lord, a home for the powerful God of Jacob.
6 We heard about it in Ephratah; we found it in the wooded fields.
7 We'll go to his tents; we'll worship at his footstool.
8 Rise up, O Lord, to your resting place; you and the chest of your power.
9 Let your priests be dressed in goodness; and let your people shout for joy.
10 For the sake of your servant David, don't turn away from your anointed king.
11 The Lord has truly promised David; he won't go back on it. I'll put one of your own descendants on your throne.
12 If your children keep my agreement and the teachings I'll give them, their children will also sit on your throne forever.
13 The Lord has chosen Zion; he wants it as his home.
14 This is my resting place forever: I'll live here, because I want it.
15 I'll richly bless her food; I'll fill her needy with bread.
16 I'll also give her priests salvation, and her people will shout for joy.
17 There I'll make David's strength grow; I've prepared a light for my chosen king.
18 I'll cover his enemies with shame, but his crown will flourish.
