Psalm 28

1 I cry to you, Lord, my rock. Don't be silent to me. If you are silent, I will become like those who go down into the pit.
2 Hear my pleas when I cry to you, when I raise my hands toward your holy place.
3 Don't drag me away with the bad people, those who do evil. They tell their neighbors they want peace, but in their hearts they plan harm.
4 Pay them back for what they do, for their evil efforts. Give them what they deserve for the work of their hands.
5 They don't care about what the Lord does, or the things he makes. So he will destroy them and not build them up again.
6 Praise the Lord, because he has heard my pleas.
7 The Lord is my strength and my shield. I trusted him with my heart, and he helped me. So my heart is very happy. I will praise him with my song.
8 The Lord is the strength of his people, and he saves his chosen king.
9 Save your people, and bless those you call your own. Feed them, and lift them up forever.
