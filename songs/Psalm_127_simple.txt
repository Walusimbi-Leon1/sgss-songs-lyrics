Psalm 127

1 Unless the Lord builds the house, the builders work for nothing. Unless the Lord guards the city, the watchman stays awake for nothing.
2 It's useless for you to get up early and stay up late, eating food with worry. Because the Lord gives sleep to those he loves.
3 Look, children are a gift from the Lord. The babies you have are his reward.
4 Kids you have when you're young are like arrows in the hands of a strong warrior.
5 Happy is the person who has many children. They won't be ashamed, but they will talk with their enemies at the city gate.
