Psalm 94

1 Lord God, you are the one who should punish the wicked. God, you are the one who should punish them. Show yourself.
2 Rise up, you who judge the earth. Give the proud what they deserve.
3 Lord, how long will the wicked win? How long will they celebrate?
4 How long will they say mean things? All those who do evil brag about themselves.
5 They hurt your people, Lord, and harm those you care for.
6 They kill widows and foreigners, and murder orphans.
7 But they say, "The Lord won't see this. The God of Jacob doesn't care."
8 Listen, you foolish people. You silly ones, when will you become wise?
9 The one who made the ear, can't he hear? The one who formed the eye, can't he see?
10 The one who punishes the nations, won't he also correct us? The one who teaches people, doesn't he know everything?
11 The Lord knows what people think. He knows their thoughts are worthless.
12 Happy is the person you discipline, Lord, and teach from your law.
13 You give them peace during tough times, until a trap is set for the wicked.
14 The Lord won't reject his people. He won't abandon those he cares for.
15 But justice will come back to what is right. All honest people will follow it.
16 Who will stand up for me against those who do evil? Who will help me against those who do wrong?
17 If the Lord hadn't been my help, I would have been silent forever.
18 When I said, "I'm slipping," Lord, your kindness held me up.
19 When I have many thoughts inside, your comfort makes my heart happy.
20 Can a throne of evil be friends with you, making trouble into law?
21 They team up against good people, and say innocent people should die.
22 But the Lord is my protection. My God is my safe place.
23 He will bring their own evil down on them. He will cut them off because of their wickedness. Yes, the Lord our God will cut them off.
