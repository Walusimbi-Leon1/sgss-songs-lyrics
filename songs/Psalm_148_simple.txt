Psalm 148

1 Praise the Lord. Praise the Lord from the sky: praise him up high.
2 Praise him, all his angels: praise him, all his heavenly armies.
3 Praise him, sun and moon: praise him, all you shining stars.
4 Praise him, highest heavens, and you waters above the sky.
5 Let them praise the name of the Lord: because he spoke, and they were created.
6 He has also set them in place forever: he made a rule that will never change.
7 Praise the Lord from the earth, you sea monsters, and all deep oceans:
8 Fire, hail, snow, mist, and stormy winds that do what he says:
9 Mountains, and all hills; fruit trees, and all cedar trees:
10 Wild animals, and all farm animals; small crawling things, and birds:
11 Kings of the earth, and all people; princes, and all judges:
12 Young men and women; old men and children:
13 Let them praise the name of the Lord: for his name alone is great; his glory is higher than earth and sky.
14 He also makes his people strong. All his faithful ones praise them. This is especially true for the Israelites, the people close to him. Praise the Lord.
