Psalm 114

1 When Israel left Egypt, Jacob's family got away from people who spoke a strange language.
2 Judah was the Lord's special place, and Israel was the land he ruled.
3 The sea saw this and ran away. The Jordan River was pushed back.
4 The mountains jumped like big sheep, and the small hills like little lambs.
5 What happened to you, sea, that you ran away? Jordan, why were you pushed back?
6 Mountains, why did you jump like big sheep? Small hills, why did you leap like little lambs?
7 Earth, shake with fear when the Lord is near, when the God of Jacob is here.
8 He turned the rock into a pool of water, and the hard stone into a spring.
