Psalm 135

1 Praise the Lord. Praise the name of the Lord. You servants of the Lord, praise him.
2 You who stand in the Lord's house, in the courtyards of our God's house.
3 Praise the Lord, because he is good. Sing to his name, because it's nice.
4 The Lord chose Jacob for himself, and Israel to be his special treasure.
5 I know the Lord is great, and our Lord is above all other gods.
6 The Lord does whatever he wants in heaven, on earth, in the seas, and in the deep places.
7 He makes mist rise from the far ends of the earth. He makes lightning for the rain. He brings wind from his storehouses.
8 He struck down the firstborn of Egypt, both people and animals.
9 He sent signs and miracles among you, Egypt, against Pharaoh and all his servants.
10 He struck great nations and killed powerful kings.
11 Sihon king of the Amorites, Og king of Bashan, and all the kingdoms of Canaan.
12 He gave their land as a gift, as an inheritance to Israel his people.
13 Lord, your name lasts forever, and your memory continues through all generations.
14 The Lord will judge his people, and he will have mercy on his servants.
15 The idols of the nations are silver and gold, made by human hands.
16 They have mouths but can't speak; they have eyes but can't see;
17 They have ears but can't hear; and there is no breath in their mouths.
18 Those who make them are just like them; so is anyone who trusts in them.
19 Bless the Lord, house of Israel. Bless the Lord, house of Aaron.
20 Bless the Lord, house of Levi. You who respect the Lord, bless him.
21 May the Lord be praised from Zion, who lives in Jerusalem. Praise the Lord.
