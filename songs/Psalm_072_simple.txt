Psalm 72

1 God, give the king your fair decisions, and give the king’s son your goodness.
2 He will judge your people with what is right, and treat the poor fairly.
3 The mountains and hills will bring peace to the people through good and right living.
4 He will speak up for the poor, save the children of those in need, and crush those who hurt others.
5 People will honor you as long as the sun and moon exist, for every generation.
6 He will come like rain on freshly cut grass, like showers that water the ground.
7 While he is king, good people will do well, and there will be peace as long as the moon shines.
8 He will rule from one sea to the other, and from the river to the ends of the world.
9 People living in the wild will bow down to him, and his enemies will be beaten down.
10 The kings from distant lands will bring him gifts, and the kings of Sheba and Seba will give presents.
11 Yes, all kings will bow before him, and every nation will serve him.
12 He will save those in need when they cry out, including the poor and those with no one to help them.
13 He will care for the poor and needy, and save their lives.
14 He will rescue them from lies and violence, and he will see their lives as precious.
15 He will live long. He will be given gold from Sheba. People will pray for him all the time, and praise him every day.
16 There will be grain even on the mountaintops, and it will flourish like the trees of Lebanon. The people in the towns will thrive like grass on the ground.
17 His name will last forever, as long as the sun. People will be blessed because of him, and all nations will call him blessed.
18 Praise the Lord, the God of Israel, who alone does amazing things.
19 Praise his wonderful name forever. Let the whole earth be full of his glory. Amen, amen.
20 This is the end of the prayers of David, son of Jesse.
