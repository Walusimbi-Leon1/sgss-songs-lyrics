Psalm 43

1 God, decide what is right for me and argue my case against a wicked nation. Rescue me from the lying and unfair person.
2 You are the God who gives me strength. Why have you pushed me away? Why do I go around sad because the enemy is crushing me?
3 Send your light and your truth. Let them guide me and bring me to your holy hill and to your temple.
4 Then I will go to God's altar, to God who is my great joy. I will praise you with my harp, my God.
5 Why are you so sad, my soul? Why are you troubled inside me? Hope in God, because I will still praise him. He is the one who gives me a smile, and he is my God.
