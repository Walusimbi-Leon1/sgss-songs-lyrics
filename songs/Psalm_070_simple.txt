Psalm 70

1 God, hurry to rescue me. Hurry to help me, Lord.
2 Make those who want to hurt me feel ashamed and confused. Make them turn back and be confused, those who wish me harm.
3 Make those who say "Aha, aha" turn back and be ashamed as they deserve.
4 May all who seek you be happy in you. And may those who love your rescue always say, "God is great!"
5 But I am poor and in need. God, hurry to me. You are my helper and rescuer. Lord, don't delay.
