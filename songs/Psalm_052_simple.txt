Psalm 52

1 Why do you brag about the bad things you do, you strong man? God's goodness lasts forever.

2 Your tongue plans trouble; it's like a sharp razor, slicing with lies.

3 You love evil more than good, and you'd rather lie than tell what's right. Selah.

4 You love all words that hurt people, you deceitful tongue.

5 God will also destroy you forever. He will take you away, pull you out of where you live, and root you out of the land of the living. Selah.

6 Those who follow God will see this, be in awe, and laugh at that man.

7 Look, this is the man who didn't rely on God for strength. He trusted in his riches and made himself strong through his evil.

8 But I am like a green olive tree in God's house. I trust in his mercy forever and ever.

9 I will praise you forever because you did this. I will trust in your name, because it is good among those who love you.
