Psalm 121

1 I lift my eyes to the hills. That's where my help comes from.
2 My help comes from the Lord. He made heaven and earth.
3 He won't let you stumble. The one who watches over you never dozes off.
4 Look, the one who watches over Israel never sleeps, not even a wink.
5 The Lord is your protector. The Lord is like shade on your right side.
6 The sun won't hurt you during the day. The moon won't hurt you at night.
7 The Lord will keep you safe from all bad things. He will protect your life.
8 The Lord will keep you safe when you go out and when you come in. He will do this from now on, and forever.
