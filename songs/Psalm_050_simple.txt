Psalm 50

1 The mighty God, even the Lord, has spoken. He called the earth from where the sun rises to where it sets.
2 God has shined out from Zion, the most beautiful place.
3 Our God is coming, and he won't stay quiet. A fire will burn in front of him, and a big storm will rage around him.
4 He will call to the heavens above and to the earth below, to judge his people.
5 Gather my faithful people to me, those who made an agreement with me through sacrifice.
6 The heavens will show his goodness, because God himself is the judge. Selah.
7 Listen, my people, I will speak. Israel, I will tell you your wrongs: I am God, your God.
8 I won't scold you for your sacrifices or offerings you burn, which you always bring to me.
9 I won't take a bull from your house or goats from your pens.
10 Every animal in the forest is mine, and all the cattle on a thousand hills.
11 I know all the birds of the mountains, and the wild animals of the fields are mine.
12 If I were hungry, I wouldn't tell you, because the world is mine and everything in it.
13 Would I eat the meat of bulls or drink the blood of goats?
14 Offer thanks to God, and keep your promises to the Most High.
15 Call to me when you're in trouble. I will rescue you, and you will honor me.
16 But to the wicked, God says: Why do you talk about my laws, or say my agreement with your lips?
17 You hate my teaching and throw my words away.
18 When you saw a thief, you agreed with him, and you joined with cheaters.
