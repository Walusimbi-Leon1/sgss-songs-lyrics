Psalm 129

1 Many times they hurt me when I was young. Let Israel say this now:
2 Many times they hurt me when I was young, but they never defeated me.
3 They plowed on my back like farmers, leaving long furrows.
4 The Lord is fair and good. He has snapped the ropes of the wicked.
5 Let everyone who hates Zion be confused and forced to go back.
6 Let them be like grass on a roof that dries up before it grows.
7 No one cutting grass picks it up, and no one gathering bundles carries it.
8 People walking by don't say, "May the Lord bless you." But we bless you in the Lord's name.
