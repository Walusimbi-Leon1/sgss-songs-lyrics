Psalm 97

1 The Lord is King. Let the earth be happy. Let all the islands be glad about it.
2 Clouds and darkness surround him. His throne is built on what is right and just.
3 Fire goes ahead of him and burns up his enemies all around.
4 His lightning lights up the world. The earth sees it and shakes.
5 The hills melt like wax when the Lord is near, the Lord of the whole earth.
6 The skies show that he is right, and everyone sees his glory.
7 May all who serve carved idols and brag about them be put to shame. All you gods, worship him.
8 Zion heard and was happy. The people of Judah were glad because of your fair judgments, Lord.
9 Lord, you are above all the earth. You are lifted up high above all gods.
10 If you love the Lord, hate evil. He keeps safe the lives of his people. He saves them from the wicked.
11 Light is planted for those who do right, and joy for those with honest hearts.
12 Be happy in the Lord, you who do right. Give thanks when you remember how holy he is.
