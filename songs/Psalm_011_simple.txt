Psalm 11

1 I trust in the Lord. Why do you tell me to flee like a bird to the mountains?
2 See, the wicked are bending their bows. They put their arrows on the string to secretly shoot at good-hearted people.
3 If the foundations are destroyed, what can the good people do?
4 The Lord is in his holy temple. The Lord’s throne is in heaven. His eyes watch, and he tests the people.
5 The Lord tests the good people, but he hates the wicked and those who love violence.
6 He will rain down traps, fire, sulfur, and a terrible storm on the wicked. That will be their share.
7 For the Lord is righteous and loves what is right. He looks with favor on those with honest hearts.
