Psalm 26

1 Lord, judge me. I have lived with honesty. I have trusted in the Lord. So I will not fall.
2 Lord, examine me and test me. Test my heart and my mind.
3 Your love and kindness are before my eyes. I have walked in your truth.
4 I have not sat with worthless people, and I will not go with fakes.
5 I hate the community of evil doers. I will not sit with the wicked.
6 I will wash my hands to show I am innocent. Then I will go around your altar, Lord.
7 So I can shout thanks, and tell of all your amazing works.
8 Lord, I love your house where you live, and the place where your glory dwells.
9 Do not take my soul with sinners, or my life with violent men.
10 They do harm with their hands, and their right hand is full of bribes.
11 But I will walk in my integrity. Save me and be merciful to me.
12 My foot stands on level ground. In the communities I will praise the Lord.
