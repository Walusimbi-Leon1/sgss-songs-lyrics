Psalm 54

1 God, save me because of who you are. Use your power to prove I'm right.
2 God, hear my prayer. Listen to the words I say.
3 Strangers have risen up against me. Bullies want to destroy me. They haven't put God in front of them. Selah
4 Look, God is my helper. The Lord is with those who stand by me.
5 He will pay my enemies back for their wrong. Cut them off because you are faithful.
6 I will gladly give to you. I will thank you, Lord, because you are good.
7 He has saved me from every problem. My eyes have seen him defeat my enemies.
