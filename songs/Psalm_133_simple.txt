Psalm 133

1 Look, how good and pleasant it is when brothers live together as one!
2 It's like fine oil poured on the head. It ran down the beard, even Aaron's beard. It went down to the bottom of his clothes.
3 It's like the dew from Mount Hermon. It's also like the dew that falls on the mountains of Zion. For there the Lord gave the blessing of life forever.
