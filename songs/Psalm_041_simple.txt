Psalm 41

1 Happy is the person who cares for the poor. The Lord will rescue him when trouble comes.

2 The Lord will protect him and keep him alive. He will be blessed here on earth. You won't hand him over to his enemies.

3 The Lord will give him strength when he is sick in bed. You will care for him and make his bed in his sickness.

4 I said, "Lord, have mercy on me. Heal my soul, because I have sinned against you."

5 My enemies say bad things about me. They ask, "When will he die and be forgotten?"

6 If he comes to see me, he speaks nonsense. His heart fills with sin. When he goes out, he spreads it around.

7 Everyone who hates me whispers together against me. They plan to hurt me.
