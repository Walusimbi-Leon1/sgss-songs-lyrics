Psalm 145

1 I will praise you, my God and King. I will bless your name forever and ever.
2 I will bless you every day. I will praise your name forever and ever.
3 The Lord is great. He deserves to be praised so much. No one can fully understand how great he is.
4 One generation will tell the next about your works. They will speak of the amazing things you have done.
5 I will talk about your majestic glory. I will tell of your wonderful works.
6 People will speak of the power of your awesome deeds. I will declare your greatness.
7 They will gladly remember your great goodness. They will sing about your right ways.
8 The Lord is kind and full of compassion. He is patient and shows great mercy.
9 The Lord is good to everyone. His loving care is over all he has made.
10 Everything you have made will praise you, Lord. Those who love you will bless you.
11 They will speak of the glory of your kingdom. They will talk about your power.
12 They make known to all people his mighty acts. They show the glorious majesty of his kingdom.
13 Your kingdom lasts forever. Your rule continues through all generations.
14 The Lord helps those who fall. He lifts up those who are bowed down.
15 All eyes look to you. You give them food at the right time.
16 You open your hand. You satisfy the needs of every living thing.
17 The Lord is right in all he does. He is holy in all his works.
18 The Lord is near to all who call on him. He is near to those who call on him sincerely.
19 He will fulfill the wishes of those who honor him. He hears their cries and saves them.
20 The Lord protects all who love him. But he will destroy the wicked.
21 My mouth will speak praise to the Lord. Let everyone bless his holy name forever and ever.
