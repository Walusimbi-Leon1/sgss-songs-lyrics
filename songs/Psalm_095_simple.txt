Psalm 95

1 Come, let's sing to the Lord. Let's shout happily to the rock who saves us.
2 Let's come to him with thankful hearts. Let's sing happy songs to him.
3 The Lord is a great God. He is a great King above all other gods.
4 He holds the deep places of the earth. The hills get their strength from him too.
5 The sea belongs to him; he made it. His hands shaped the dry land.
6 Come, let's worship and bow down. Let's kneel before the Lord who made us.
7 He is our God. We are the people in his pasture, the sheep he cares for. Today, if you hear his voice,
8 Don't harden your heart, like when you provoked me, and like that day you tested me in the wilderness.
9 When your ancestors tested me, tried me, and saw what I did.
10 For forty years I was sad because of that generation. I said, They are people who stray in their hearts. They have not known my ways.
11 I swore to them in my anger that they would not enter my rest.
