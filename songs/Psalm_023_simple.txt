Psalm 23

1 The Lord is my shepherd. I have everything I need.
2 He lets me rest in green fields. He leads me to calm water.
3 He refreshes my soul. He guides me on good paths because of his name.
4 Even when I walk through a dark valley where death is near, I won't fear any bad thing. You are with me. Your rod and staff make me feel safe.
5 You set a table for me right in front of my enemies. You pour oil on my head. My cup overflows.
6 Surely your goodness and love will follow me every day of my life. I will stay in the Lord's house forever.
