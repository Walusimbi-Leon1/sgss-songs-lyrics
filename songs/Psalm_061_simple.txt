Psalm 61

1 God, listen to my cry and pay attention to my prayer.
2 From the end of the earth I will cry out to you when my heart is overwhelmed. Lead me to the rock that is higher than me.
3 You have been a safe place for me, a strong tower protecting me from my enemy.
4 I will stay in your tent forever. I will trust in the shelter of your wings.
Selah.
5 God, you have heard my promises to you. You have given me the inheritance of those who respect your name.
6 You will give the king a long life, and his years will last through many generations.
7 He will stay with God forever. God, please show mercy and truth to keep him safe.
8 So I will sing praises to you forever, so I can keep my promises to you every day.
