Psalm 76

1 God is known in Judah. His name is great in Israel.
2 His tent is in Salem too. He makes his home in Zion.
3 There he broke the arrows, shields, and swords. He stopped the battle. Selah.
4 You are more glorious and wonderful than the mountains of prey.
5 The brave hearts are ruined. They have gone to sleep. None of the strong men could even use their hands.
6 When you scolded them, God of Jacob, both the chariots and horses fell into a dead sleep.
7 You, yes you, are the one to fear. Who can stand in front of you when you get angry?
8 You let your judgment be heard from heaven. The earth was afraid and was still,
9 when God stood up to judge and save all the gentle people on earth. Selah.
10 For sure, man's anger will praise you. You will restrain whatever anger is left.
11 Make a vow to the Lord your God and keep it. Let all who are near him bring gifts to the one who should be feared.
12 He will cut off the bold spirit of rulers. He is scary to the kings of the earth.
