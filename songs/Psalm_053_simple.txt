Psalm 53

1 A fool says deep down, "There is no God." But people are corrupt. They have done terrible wrong. No one does good.
2 God looked down from heaven on people. He checked to see if anyone understood, if anyone was looking for him.
3 Everyone has turned away. They have all become filthy. No one does good, not one.
4 Don't those who do evil have any sense? They gobble up my people like they're eating bread. They never pray to God.
5 They were scared to death where there was nothing to fear. God scattered the bones of the enemy who attacked you. You made them ashamed, because God rejected them.
6 If only God would save Israel from Zion! When he brings his people home from captivity, Jacob will be happy and Israel will be glad.
