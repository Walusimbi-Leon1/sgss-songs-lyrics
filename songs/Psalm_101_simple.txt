Psalm 101

1 I will sing about your kindness and justice. I will sing to you, O Lord.
2 I will live wisely and do what is right. Lord, when will you come to me? I will walk in my home with a true heart.
3 I will not look at anything evil. I hate what those who turn away do. It will not stick to me.
4 A dishonest heart will stay away from me. I will not be friends with anyone evil.
5 Anyone who secretly talks bad about their neighbor, I will get rid of them. I will not tolerate anyone who is arrogant and proud.
6 I will watch over the loyal people in the land, so they can live with me. Those who live rightly will serve me.
7 No one who is deceitful will live in my home. No one who lies will stay near me.
8 I will quickly remove all evil people from the land, so I can get rid of all who do wrong from the Lord's city.
