Psalm 19

1 The heavens tell of God's greatness. The sky shows what he has made.
2 Each day tells a story. Each night teaches us something.
3 They don't speak with words, but their message is heard in every language.
4 Their message goes out through all the earth. Their words reach the ends of the world. In the sky, he set a place for the sun to live.
5 The sun is like a groom coming out of his room. It is happy like a strong man eager to run.
6 The sun rises at one end of the sky. It travels across to the other end. Nothing can hide from its heat.
7 The Lord's teachings are perfect. They bring our souls back to life. The Lord's promises are trustworthy. They make simple people wise.
8 The Lord's rules are right. They make our hearts glad. The Lord's commands are pure. They open our eyes to see.
9 Respect for the Lord is pure. It lasts forever. The Lord's judgments are true and always fair.
10 They are worth more than gold, even the best gold. They are sweeter than honey straight from the comb.
11 They warn me, your servant. When I follow them, I get a great reward.
12 Who can understand their own errors? Cleanse me from secret faults.
13 Keep me, your servant, from willful sins. Don't let them control me. Then I will be honest and free from big sin.
