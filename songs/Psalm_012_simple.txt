Psalm 12

1 Lord, help! The people who love you are disappearing. The faithful ones are gone from among the people.
2 Everyone speaks nonsense to their neighbor. They flatter with their lips and are two-faced in their hearts.
3 The Lord will silence all who flatter and those who speak with pride.
4 They say, "We will win by our words. Our lips are our own. Who is the Lord over us?"
5 Because the poor are oppressed and the needy groan, I will now rise up, says the Lord. I will keep him safe from the one who threatens him.
6 The Lord's words are pure, like silver melted in a clay furnace and refined seven times.
7 Lord, you will guard them. You will keep them safe from these people forever.
8 The wicked roam all around, when the worst people are lifted up.
