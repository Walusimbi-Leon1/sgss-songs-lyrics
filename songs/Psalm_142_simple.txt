Psalm 142

1 I cried to the Lord; I asked him for help.
2 I told him what was troubling me; I showed him my problems.
3 When I felt overwhelmed inside, you knew where I was going. On the path I walked, they secretly set a trap for me.
4 I looked to my right and saw that no one knew me. I had no safe place; no one cared about me.
5 I cried to you, Lord: I said, you are my safe place and what I have among the living.
6 Listen to my cry, because I am really down. Save me from those who hurt me, for they are stronger than me.
7 Set me free from this prison, so I can praise you. Good people will gather around me, because you will be good to me.
