Psalm 66

1 Shout happily to God, all the world:
2 Sing about how great his name is: make his praise amazing.
3 Say to God, How awesome you are in what you do! Because you are so powerful, your enemies will give in to you.
4 Everyone on earth will worship you and sing to you; they will sing about your name. Selah.
5 Come and see what God has done: he is awesome in how he treats people.
6 He turned the sea into dry ground: they walked through the sea on foot: we celebrated him.
7 He rules with his power forever; he watches the nations: don't let the rebellious boast. Selah.
8 Come on, everyone, bless our God, and let his praise be heard:
9 He keeps us alive and doesn't let us stumble.
10 God, you have tested us: you have refined us like silver is refined.
11 You caught us in a trap; you laid pain on our backs.
12 You let people ride over our heads; we went through fire and water: but you brought us out to a rich place.
13 I will go to your house with offerings: I will keep my promises to you,
14 which I said with my lips when I was in trouble.
15 I will bring you fat animals as offerings, with ram incense; I will bring cattle and goats. Selah.
16 Come and listen, all you who respect God, and I will tell what he did for me.
17 I called out to him with my mouth, and I praised him with my tongue.
