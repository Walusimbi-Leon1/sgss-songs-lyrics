Psalm 51

1 God, be merciful to me because of your love. By your great mercy, wipe away my sins.
2 Wash me completely from my sin, and make me clean from my wrongdoing.
3 I admit my sins, and my sin is always in front of me.
4 I have sinned against you alone, and done evil in your sight. So when you speak, you are right, and when you judge, you are fair.
5 I was formed in sin; my mother conceived me in sin.
6 You want truth in my heart. Deep inside, you will teach me wisdom.
7 Cleanse me with hyssop, and I will be clean. Wash me, and I'll be whiter than snow.
8 Let me hear joy and happiness, so my broken bones can rejoice.
9 Look away from my sins, and erase all my wrongdoing.
10 God, make in me a clean heart, and renew a right spirit inside me.
11 Don't push me away from you, and don't take your Holy Spirit from me.
12 Bring back the joy of your salvation to me, and support me with your free spirit.
13 Then I will teach wrongdoers your ways, and sinners will turn to you.
14 God, my saving God, deliver me from the guilt of bloodshed, and my tongue will sing loudly of your righteousness.
15 Lord, open my lips, and my mouth will praise you.
16 You don't want sacrifices, or I would give them. You don't take joy in burnt offerings.
17 What God wants as sacrifice is a broken spirit. A broken and sorry heart, God, you will not reject.
18 Please do good for Zion; build the walls of Jerusalem.
19 Then you will be pleased with right sacrifices, with burnt offerings and whole burnt offerings. Then they will offer bulls on your altar.
