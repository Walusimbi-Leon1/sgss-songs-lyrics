Psalm 13

1 Lord, how long will you forget me? Forever? How long will you turn away from me?
2 How long must I worry in my mind and feel sad every day? How long will my enemy keep winning over me?
3 Lord my God, pay attention and listen to me. Brighten my eyes, or I will die.
4 Or else my enemy will say, "I beat him." And those who hurt me will cheer when I fall.
5 But I trust in your love. My heart will be glad because you rescue me.
6 I will sing to the Lord, because he has been so good to me.
