Song of Solomon 3

1 At night, I looked for the one my heart loves while lying in bed. I looked for him, but I couldn't find him.
2 So I got up and walked through the city. I went down the streets and through the open squares looking for the one I love. I looked for him, but I couldn't find him.
3 The guards walking around the city saw me. I asked them, "Have you seen the one my heart loves?"
4 I had just walked away from them when I found the one I love. I grabbed him and held on tight. I didn't let go until I brought him to my mother's home, to the room of the woman who gave birth to me.
5 I beg you, women of Jerusalem, just as surely as there are deer and gazelles in the fields, please don't wake up or disturb my love until he is ready.
6 Look, who is that coming from the desert like columns of smoke? He smells sweet like myrrh and incense, like all the spices a merchant sells.
7 Look at his bed. It belongs to Solomon. Sixty brave soldiers surround it. They are the strongest men in Israel.
8 They all carry swords and know how to fight. Each man has his sword at his side because of danger at night.
9 King Solomon built a carriage made from wood from Lebanon.
10 He made its posts out of silver and its base out of gold. He covered it with purple cloth. He decorated the inside with love, all for the women of Jerusalem.
11 Come out, women of Zion, and look at King Solomon. See the crown his mother put on his head on his wedding day, the day his heart was full of joy.
