Psalm 46

1 God is our safe place and our strength, always ready to help when we're in trouble.
2 So we won't be afraid, even if the earth is shaken, and even if mountains fall into the middle of the sea.
3 Even if its waters crash and foam, even if mountains tremble at the rising waves. Selah.
4 There is a river whose streams will bring joy to the city of God, the holy place where the Most High lives.
5 God is right in the middle of that city; it will not be moved. God will help it, and help will come quickly.
6 The nations raged, the kingdoms were shaken. But God spoke, and the earth melted.
7 The Lord of armies is with us; the God of Jacob is our safe place. Selah.
8 Come, look at the works of the Lord, the ruins he has made in the earth.
9 He stops wars everywhere on earth. He breaks the bows, snaps the spears in two, and burns the chariots in the fire.
10 Just be still and know that I am God. I will be honored among the nations, I will be honored in the whole earth.
11 The Lord of armies is with us; the God of Jacob is our safe place. Selah.
