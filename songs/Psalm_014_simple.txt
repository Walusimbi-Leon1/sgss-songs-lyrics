Psalm 14

1 The fool says in his heart, "There is no God." They are rotten, they have done terrible things, no one does good.
2 The Lord looked down from heaven on people, to see if anyone understood and looked for God.
3 They have all turned away, they have all become dirty: no one does good, not even one.
4 Don't all those who do evil know better? They eat my people like bread, and don't call on the Lord.
5 They were very afraid, because God is with those who do right.
6 You have shamed the plans of the poor, because the Lord is their safe place.
7 I wish the Lord would save Israel from Zion! When the Lord brings his people back from being captured, Jacob will be happy and Israel will be glad.
