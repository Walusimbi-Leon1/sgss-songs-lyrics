Psalm 40

1 I waited patiently for the Lord. He heard my cry and listened to me.
2 He pulled me out of a terrible pit, out of the mud. He set my feet on a rock and made my steps steady.
3 He put a new song in my mouth, a song of praise to our God. Many will see this and be amazed, and they'll trust in the Lord.
4 Happy is the person who trusts in the Lord. They don't look up to the proud or follow those who tell lies.
5 Lord my God, you have done so many wonderful things. Your plans for us are countless. If I tried to talk about them all, there would be more than I could count.
6 You don't want sacrifices and offerings. You opened my ears to hear you. You don't require burnt offerings or sin offerings.
7 Then I said, "Look, I am coming. The book says this is written about me."
8 I love to do what you want, my God. Your teachings are deep in my heart.
9 I have spoken about your goodness to the whole community. I didn't keep my lips closed, Lord, you know that.
10 I haven't kept your goodness inside me. I have told others about your faithfulness and your saving power. I haven't hidden your love and truth from the community.
11 Please don't hold back your mercy from me, Lord. Let your love and truth always protect me.
