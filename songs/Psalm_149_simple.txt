Psalm 149

1 Praise the Lord. Sing a new song to the Lord. Praise him in the community of his people.
2 Let Israel be happy in the one who made them. Let the people of Zion be glad in their King.
3 Let them praise his name by dancing. Let them sing to him with tambourines and harps.
4 The Lord is happy with his people. He will save the humble and make them shine.
5 Let God's people be glad in his glory. Let them sing out loud on their beds.
6 Let them speak high praise of God. Let them hold a double-edged sword in their hand.
7 They will take revenge on the nations. They will punish the peoples.
8 They will chain up their kings. They will put their leaders in iron bonds.
9 They will do what the written judgment says. All his people share this honor.
Praise the Lord.
