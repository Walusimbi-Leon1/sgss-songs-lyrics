Psalm 1

1 Happy is the person who doesn't follow the advice of the wicked, doesn't stand with sinners, and doesn't sit with those who mock others.
2 But he finds joy in the Lord's teachings, and thinks about them day and night.
3 He is like a tree planted by a stream of water. It grows fruit at the right time, and its leaves don't dry up. Everything he does goes well.
4 The wicked are not like that. They are like dry husks that the wind blows away.
5 So the wicked won't stand in the judgment, and sinners won't be with the righteous community.
6 Because the Lord knows the path of the righteous, but the path of the wicked will be destroyed.
