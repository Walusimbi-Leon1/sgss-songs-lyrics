Psalm 67

1 God, be kind to us and bless us. Let your face shine on us. Selah.
2 Then your way will be known on earth. Your saving help will be known among all nations.
3 Let the people praise you, God. Let all the people praise you.
4 Let the nations be glad and sing for joy. You will judge the people fairly. You will rule the nations on earth. Selah.
5 Let the people praise you, God. Let all the people praise you.
6 Then the earth will give its crops. God, our own God, will bless us.
7 God will bless us. Everyone everywhere will respect him.
