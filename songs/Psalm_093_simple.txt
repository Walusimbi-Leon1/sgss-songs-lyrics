Psalm 93

1 The Lord is king. He wears majesty like a robe. The Lord is dressed in strength, and he put that strength on himself. The world is set firm and cannot be moved.
2 Your throne has been set up from ancient times. You have always existed.
3 The floods rose up, Lord. The floods raised their voices. The floods lifted their waves.
4 The Lord above is stronger than the roar of many waters. He is even stronger than the huge waves of the sea.
5 Your words are completely trustworthy. Holiness is right for your house, Lord, forever.
