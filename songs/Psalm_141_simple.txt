Psalm 141

1 Lord, I call out to you. Hurry to me. Listen to my voice when I call out to you.
2 May my prayer be like incense before you. May my lifted hands be like the evening sacrifice.
3 Lord, put a watch over my mouth. Guard my lips.
4 Don't turn my heart to any evil thing, to do wicked deeds with those who do wrong. And don't let me eat their tasty treats.
5 Let good people correct me; that is a kindness. Let them rebuke me; it will be like good oil that won't hurt my head. Even when they have troubles, I will still pray.
6 When their leaders are thrown down in rocky places, they will hear my words, for they are kind.
7 Our bones are scattered at the edge of the grave, like wood chips when someone cuts and splits wood on the ground.
8 But my eyes are on you, O God the Lord. I trust in you. Don't leave me helpless.
9 Keep me from the traps they have set for me, and the snares of those who do evil.
10 Let the wicked fall into their own nets, while I escape.
