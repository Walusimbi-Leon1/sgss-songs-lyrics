Psalm 84

1 How lovely are your houses, O Lord of hosts!
2 My soul longs to be there. I even feel faint for the courts of the Lord. My heart and my body cry out for the living God.
3 Look, the little sparrow has found a home, and the swallow a nest to raise her young, right by your altars, O Lord of hosts, my King and my God.
4 Happy are those who live in your house. They never stop praising you. Selah.
5 Happy is the one who gets strength from you, and who keeps your ways in their heart.
6 As they go through the valley of tears, they make it a spring. The rain fills the pools.
7 They grow stronger and stronger. Each of them appears before God in Zion.
8 O Lord God of hosts, please hear my prayer. Listen to me, O God of Jacob. Selah.
9 Look, O God our shield, and look on the face of your anointed one.
10 A single day in your courtyards is better than a thousand anywhere else. I'd rather be a doorkeeper in my God's house than live in the homes of the wicked.
11 For the Lord God is like a sun and a shield. The Lord gives favor and honor. He will not hold back any good thing from those who live rightly.
12 O Lord of hosts, happy is the person who trusts in you.
