Psalm 3

1 Lord, so many people are troubling me! Many are rising up against me.
2 Many say about me, "God won't help him." Selah.
3 But you, Lord, are like a shield for me. You are my glory, and you lift my head high.
4 I called out to the Lord, and he heard me from his holy hill. Selah.
5 I lay down and slept. I woke up, because the Lord kept me safe.
6 I won't be afraid of tens of thousands of people who surround me and oppose me.
7 Rise up, Lord! Save me, my God! You have struck all my enemies on the cheek. You have broken the teeth of the wicked.
8 Salvation comes from the Lord. Your blessing is on your people. Selah.
