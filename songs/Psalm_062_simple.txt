Psalm 62

1 Truly I wait on God. My salvation comes from him.
2 He alone is my rock and my salvation. He is my protection. I will not be shaken.
3 How long will you plan trouble against a man? You will all be killed. You are like a leaning wall, like a wobbly fence.
4 They get together just to bring him down from his high place. They love lies. They say good things with their mouths, but inside they curse him. Selah.
5 I wait only on God. My hope is in him.
6 He alone is my rock and my salvation. He is my protection. I will not be moved.
7 God is my salvation and my glory. He is the rock of my strength and my safe place.
8 Trust him at all times. You all, pour out your hearts to him. God is our safe place. Selah.
9 Truly, lowly people are worthless, and important people are fake. If you weigh them, they are all lighter than nothing.
10 Don't trust in oppressing others, and don't get proud from robbery. If your riches increase, don't set your heart on them.
11 God has spoken once. I have heard it twice: power belongs to God.
12 Lord, mercy also belongs to you. For you repay everyone for what they have done.
