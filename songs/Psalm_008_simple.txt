Psalm 8

1 Lord, our Lord, how wonderful your name is everywhere on earth! You have placed your glory above the heavens.

2 You have given strength from the mouths of babies and infants. You did this because of your enemies, to silence those who fight you and want revenge.

3 When I look at the heavens you made, the moon and stars you put in place.

4 What are people that you think about them? What are humans that you care for them?

5 You made them just a little lower than angels, and crowned them with glory and honor.

6 You let them rule over everything you made; you put all things under their feet:

7 all sheep and cows, and also the wild animals;

8 the birds in the sky, the fish in the sea, and everything that moves through the ocean.

9 Lord, our Lord, how wonderful your name is everywhere on earth!
