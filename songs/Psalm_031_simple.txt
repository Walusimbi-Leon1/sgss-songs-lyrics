Psalm 31

1 I trust you, Lord. Please don't let me be ashamed. Save me because you are righteous.
2 Listen to me, Lord. Hurry and save me. Be my strong rock, a safe place to protect me.
3 You are my rock and my fortress. So lead me and guide me, for the sake of your name.
4 Pull me out of the trap they set secretly for me, because you are my strength.
5 I put my life in your hands. You have rescued me, Lord, faithful God.
6 I hate those who trust in worthless lies, but I trust in the Lord.
7 I will be happy and rejoice in your kindness, because you have seen my trouble and known my struggles.
8 You didn't hand me over to my enemy. You gave me room to breathe.
9 Have mercy on me, Lord, because I'm in trouble. My eyes are worn out from sadness, and so are my soul and body.
10 My life is full of sorrow, and my years with groaning. My strength is gone because of my sins, and my bones are weak.
11 My enemies mocked me, especially my neighbors. My friends were afraid of me. Those who saw me ran away.
12 I'm forgotten like a dead person no one remembers. I'm like a broken pot.
13 I've heard many people spread lies about me. Fear was all around. They got together and planned to kill me.
14 But I trusted you, Lord. I said, "You are my God."
15 My life is in your hands. Save me from my enemies and those who chase me.
16 Smile on me, your servant. Save me because you are merciful.
17 Don't let me be ashamed, Lord, because I've called to you. Let the wicked be ashamed and silent in death.
18 Silence those lying mouths that proudly say cruel things against good people.
19 How great is your goodness, stored up for those who respect you, shown to those who trust you in front of others!
20 You hide them in your presence from proud people. You keep them safe in your shelter from hurtful talk.
21 Praise the Lord! He has shown me his amazing kindness in a strong city.
22 I said in my panic, "I'm cut off from you." But you heard my cries for help when I called to you.
23 Love the Lord, all you his people, because he protects the faithful and gives the proud what they deserve.
24 Be brave, and he will strengthen your heart, all you who hope in the Lord.
