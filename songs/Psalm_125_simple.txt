Psalm 125

1 Those who trust in the Lord are like Mount Zion. They cannot be moved, and they stay forever.
2 Just as the mountains are all around Jerusalem, the Lord is all around his people, now and forever.
3 The wicked will not keep their power over the righteous. This is so the righteous don't reach out to do evil.
4 Lord, please do good to those who are good, and to those who are honest in their hearts.
5 Those who turn aside to their crooked ways, the Lord will lead them away with those who do evil. But peace will be with Israel.
