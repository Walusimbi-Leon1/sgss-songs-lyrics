Psalm 100

1 Shout with joy to the Lord, all the earth.
2 Serve the Lord with joy. Come to him singing.
3 Know that the Lord is God. He made us; we didn't make ourselves. We are his people, the sheep of his pasture.
4 Enter his gates with thanks, and his courts with praise. Thank him and praise his name.
5 The Lord is good. His love never ends, and his truth lasts for all generations.
