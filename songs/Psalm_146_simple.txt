Psalm 146

1 Praise the Lord. My soul, praise the Lord.
2 As long as I live, I will praise the Lord. I will sing to my God while I am alive.
3 Don't trust in rulers or in any human, because they can't really help you.
4 When they stop breathing, they return to the earth. On that very day, their thoughts die.
5 Happy is the one who has the God of Jacob as their helper, whose hope is in the Lord their God.
6 He made the heavens, the earth, the sea, and everything in them. He keeps his promises forever.
7 He makes things right for those who are treated badly. He gives food to the hungry. The Lord sets prisoners free.
8 The Lord opens the eyes of the blind. The Lord lifts up those who are bent over. The Lord loves those who do what is right.
9 The Lord protects foreigners. He cares for orphans and widows. But he turns the way of the wicked upside down.
10 The Lord will rule forever, your God, O Zion, for all generations. Praise the Lord.
