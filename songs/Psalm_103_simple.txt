Psalm 103

1 Praise the Lord, my soul, and everything inside me, praise his holy name.
2 Praise the Lord, my soul, and don't forget all the good things he does for you.
3 He forgives all your sins; he heals all your sicknesses.
4 He saves your life from ruin; he wraps you in love and gentle mercy.
5 He fills your mouth with good food, so your youth comes back like an eagle's.
