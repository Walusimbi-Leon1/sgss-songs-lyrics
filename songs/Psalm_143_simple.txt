Psalm 143

1 Lord, hear my prayer. Listen to my requests. Answer me because you are faithful and always do what is right.
2 Lord, please don't judge me, your servant. No one living can be seen as right in your eyes.
3 My enemy has chased me and hurt me deep inside. He has knocked my life to the ground. He has left me living in darkness, like those who died long ago.
4 So my spirit is overwhelmed inside me. My heart feels empty and alone.
5 I remember the days long ago. I think about all the things you have done. I ponder the work of your hands.
6 I reach my hands out to you. My soul is thirsty for you, like dry ground thirsty for water. Selah.
7 Lord, hear me soon. My spirit is giving out
