Psalm 126

1 When the Lord brought Zion's captives back, we were like people dreaming.
2 We laughed and sang. Then the nations said, "The Lord has done great things for them."
3 The Lord has done great things for us. We are happy.
4 Lord, bring our captives back, like streams in the dry south.
5 Those who plant with tears will harvest with joy.
6 Anyone who goes out weeping, carrying good seed, will surely come back happy, bringing their harvest.
