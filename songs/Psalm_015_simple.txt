Psalm 15

1 Lord, who can stay in your house? Who can live on your holy mountain?
2 The one who lives with integrity, does what is right, and speaks truth from the heart.
3 He doesn't gossip about others, doesn't harm his neighbor, and doesn't join in blaming his neighbor.
4 He looks down on a bad person, but he respects those who fear the Lord. He keeps his promise even when it hurts him, and doesn't change it.
5 He doesn't lend money for interest, and doesn't take bribes to hurt the innocent. Whoever does these things will never be shaken.
