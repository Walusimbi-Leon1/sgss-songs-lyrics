Psalm 39

1 I said, I will watch my ways so I won't sin with my words. I will keep my mouth under control like a horse with a bridle while wicked people are around me.

2 I stayed completely silent. I didn't say anything, not even good things. And my sadness grew inside me.

3 My heart was burning inside me while I was thinking. Then I spoke out loud.

4 Lord, help me know when I will die and how long my life will be. Let me see how weak and short my life is.

5 Look, you have made my days as short as a hand's width. My whole life is nothing compared to you. Truly, even the best person is completely meaningless. Selah.

6 Truly, everyone lives like an empty shadow. They worry for nothing. They pile up wealth but don't know who will collect it later.

7 Now, Lord, what am I waiting for? My hope is in you.

8 Save me from all my sins. Don't let me become a joke to fools.

9 I was silent. I didn't open my mouth, because you are the one who did this.

10 Take your punishment away from me. I am being destroyed by the blow of your hand.

11 When you scold and correct a person for their sin, you make their strength waste away like cloth eaten by moths. Truly, every person is meaningless. Selah.

12 Hear my prayer, O Lord, and listen to my cry. Don't stay silent when I cry. I am like a stranger and a traveler with you, just like all my ancestors were.

13 O spare me, so I can regain my strength before I leave this life and am gone.
