Psalm 20

1 May the Lord hear you when you're in trouble. May the name of the God of Jacob protect you.
2 May he send you help from his holy place, and give you strength from Zion.
3 May he remember all the gifts you bring, and accept your burned offerings.
4 May he give you what your heart desires, and make all your plans succeed.
5 We will celebrate because you saved us, and we will raise our flags in the name of our God. May the Lord answer all your prayers.
6 Now I know that the Lord saves his chosen king. He hears him from his holy heaven with the power of his right hand.
7 Some trust in chariots and some in horses, but we trust in the name of the Lord our God.
8 They have fallen down, but we have gotten up and stand tall.
9 Save us, Lord! Let the king hear us when we cry out.
