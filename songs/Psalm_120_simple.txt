Psalm 120

1 When I was in trouble, I cried to the Lord, and he listened to me.
2 Lord, save me from people who lie and from those who speak deceitfully.
3 What should be given to you? What should be done to you, you liar?
4 You'll get sharp arrows from the strong, and burning coals of juniper.
5 I'm so sad that I live far away in Mesech, in the tents of Kedar!
6 I've lived a long time with someone who hates peace.
7 I want peace, but when I talk, they want war.
