Psalm 6

1 Lord, please don't scold me when you are angry, and don't punish me when you are really upset.
2 Lord, be kind to me because I am weak. Lord, heal me, my bones hurt.
3 My soul is also very troubled. But you, Lord, how long?
4 Come back, Lord, and save me. Rescue my soul. Oh save me because you are merciful.
5 In death, no one remembers you. In the grave, who can thank you?
6 I'm tired from groaning. All night I cry so much my bed gets soaked. I wet my couch with tears.
7 My eyes are worn out from sadness. They get old because of all my enemies.
8 Get away from me, all you who do wrong, because the Lord has heard my crying.
9 The Lord has heard my request. The Lord will take my prayer.
10 May all my enemies be embarrassed and really troubled. May they turn and be ashamed right away.
