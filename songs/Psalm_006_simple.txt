Psalm 6

1 Lord, don't scold me when you're angry, and don't punish me when you're really upset.
2 Lord, have mercy on me because I am weak. Lord, heal me, my bones hurt badly.
3 I am also deeply troubled. But Lord, how long will this last?
4 Lord, come and save me. Rescue me because you are merciful.
5 If I die, I can't remember you. In the grave, who can thank you?
6 I'm tired from groaning. All night I cry, and my bed is soaked with tears.
7 My eyes are worn out from sadness. They are tired because of all my enemies.
8 Go away, all you who do evil, because the Lord has heard me crying.
9 The Lord has heard my plea. The Lord will accept my prayer.
10 May all my enemies be embarrassed and troubled. May they turn back and be ashamed quickly.
