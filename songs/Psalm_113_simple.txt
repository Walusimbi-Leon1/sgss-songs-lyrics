Psalm 113

1 Praise the Lord! You who serve the Lord, praise his name.
2 The Lord's name should be praised from now and forever.
3 From sunrise to sunset, we should praise the Lord's name.
4 The Lord is higher than all nations, and his glory is above the heavens.
5 No one is like the Lord our God, who lives on high.
6 He lowers himself to see what's in heaven and on earth.
7 He raises the poor from the dust and lifts the needy from the trash pile.
8 He does this to seat them with the leaders of his people.
9 He helps the woman with no children to have a home and become a happy mother.
Praise the Lord.
