Psalm 98

1 Sing a new song to the Lord. He has done amazing things. His right hand and his holy arm have won the victory for him.
2 The Lord has made his rescue known. He has openly shown his righteousness to the nations.
3 He has remembered his mercy and his faithfulness to the people of Israel. Everyone on earth has seen our God's rescue.
4 Everyone on earth, make a happy noise to the Lord. Be loud, rejoice, and sing praise to him.
5 Sing to the Lord with the harp. Use the harp and a song of praise.
6 Use trumpets and horns to make a happy noise before the Lord, the King.
7 Let the sea roar and everything in it. Let the world and all who live in it rejoice.
8 Let the rivers clap their hands. Let the hills be happy together.
9 Before the Lord, because he comes to judge the earth. He will judge the world with righteousness, and the people with fairness.
