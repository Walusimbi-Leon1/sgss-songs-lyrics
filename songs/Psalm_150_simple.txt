Psalm 150

1 Praise the Lord. Praise God in his holy place. Praise him in the sky where he shows his power.
2 Praise him for the powerful things he does. Praise him because he is so great.
3 Praise him with trumpets. Praise him with harps and stringed instruments.
4 Praise him with tambourines and dancing. Praise him with stringed instruments and organs.
5 Praise him with loud cymbals. Praise him with crashing cymbals.
6 Everything that breathes should praise the Lord. Praise the Lord.
