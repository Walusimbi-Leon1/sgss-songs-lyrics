Psalm 124

1 If the Lord had not been on our side, let Israel say:
2 If the Lord had not been on our side when people attacked us:
3 Then they would have gobbled us up right away when they got mad at us.
4 Then the floods would have overwhelmed us, the stream would have washed over our lives.
5 Then the raging waters would have washed over our lives.
6 Thank the Lord, who did not hand us over to be chewed up like prey.
7 We have escaped like a bird from a trap set by bird hunters. The trap is broken, and we are free.
8 Our help is in the name of the Lord, who made heaven and earth.
