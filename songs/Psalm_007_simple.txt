Psalm 7

1 Lord my God, I trust in you. Save me from everyone who is hurting me, and rescue me.
2 Otherwise, he will tear me apart like a lion, ripping me to pieces, and no one will rescue me.
3 Lord my God, if I have done this wrong thing; if I have sinned with my hands;
4 if I have paid back evil to someone who was friendly to me; (yes, I have even helped my enemy who had no reason to hate me:)
5 then let my enemy chase me and take my life; let him crush me on the ground and throw my honor in the dirt. Selah.
6 Get up, Lord, be angry at my enemies, rise up because they rage against me. Wake up and bring the fair judgment you commanded.
7 Then all the people will gather around you. For their sake, rise up high again.
