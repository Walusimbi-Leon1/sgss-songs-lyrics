Psalm 79

1 God, the nations have come into the land you gave us. They have dirtied your holy temple. They have left Jerusalem in piles of rubble.

2 They have left your servants' dead bodies for the birds to eat. They have fed your people's flesh to the wild animals.

3 They spilled their blood like water all around Jerusalem. No one was there to bury them.

4 We have become a shame to our neighbors. Those around us make fun of us and mock us.

5 Lord, how long will you be angry with us? Will your jealousy keep burning like fire forever?

6 Pour out your anger on the nations that don't know you. Pour it on the kingdoms that have not called on your name.

7 They have destroyed your people Jacob and ruined their homes.

8 God, don't hold our past sins against us. Let your gentle mercy reach us soon, for we are really down and helpless.

9 Help us, God who saves us, for the honor of your name. Rescue us and wash away our sins, for your name's sake.

10 Why should the nations say, 'Where is their God?' Show yourself to the nations by punishing them for the blood of your servants they spilled. Let us see it too.

11 Let the groans of prisoners reach you. By your great power, protect those who are sentenced to die.

12 Pay back our neighbors seven times over for the shame they brought on you, Lord. Let that shame fall on them.

13 Then we, your people and your flock, will thank you forever. We will sing your praises to every generation.
