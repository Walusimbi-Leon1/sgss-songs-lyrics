Song of Solomon 7

1 Your feet look so beautiful in those sandals, my princess! Your thighs are like fine jewels, made by a skilled craftsman.
2 Your navel is like a round bowl that never runs out of wine. Your tummy is like a pile of wheat with lilies all around it.
3 Your two breasts are like twin fawns, young and gentle.
4 Your neck is like a smooth ivory tower. Your eyes are like calm pools of water in Heshbon, near the Bathrabbim gate. Your nose is like a tower on Lebanon looking toward Damascus.
5 Your head is like Carmel mountain. Your hair is like purple cloth. The king is taken by your beauty.
6 How lovely and pleasant you are, my love, full of delight!
7 Your body is tall like a palm tree. Your breasts are like clusters of grapes.
8 I said, I'll go up to that palm tree and grab its branches. Your breasts are like grape bunches, and your breath smells sweet like apples.
9 The taste of your mouth is like fine wine for my dear, it slides down smoothly, making sleepy lips want to speak.
10 I belong to my love, and he wants me.
11 Come, my love, let's go out to the fields. Let's stay in the villages.
12 Let's wake up early and go to the vineyards. Let's see if the vines are growing, if the grapes are showing, and if pomegranates are budding. There I will give you my love.
13 The mandrakes smell sweet, and at our gate are all kinds of good fruits, fresh and stored, that I've kept for you, my love.
