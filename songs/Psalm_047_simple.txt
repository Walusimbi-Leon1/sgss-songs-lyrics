Psalm 47

1 Come on, everyone, clap your hands! Shout to God with a shout of joy.
2 The Lord is the Most High, and he is awesome. He is a great King over the whole earth.
3 He will put the people under our rule, and the nations under our feet.
4 He will choose what we will inherit, the best of Jacob's family, whom he loved.
Selah.
5 God has gone up with a loud cheer, the Lord with the sound of a trumpet.
6 Sing to God, sing! Sing to our King, sing!
7 God is the King of the whole world. So sing your songs of praise with thoughtfulness.
8 God rules over all nations. He sits on his holy throne.
9 The leaders of the people come together, even the people of the God of Abraham. The defenders of the earth belong to God, and he is lifted up high.
