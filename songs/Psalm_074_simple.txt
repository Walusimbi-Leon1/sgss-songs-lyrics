Psalm 74

1 God, why have you left us forever? Why are you so angry with your people, the sheep in your pasture?

2 Remember your community that you bought long ago. Remember the people you claimed as your own and saved. Remember Zion, the hill where you lived.

3 Come to the ruins that are always there. See all the bad things the enemy did in your holy place.

4 Your enemies shout in the middle of your gatherings. They put up their flags as signs of victory.

5 Long ago, men were known for swinging axes at the big trees.

6 But now they are smashing its carvings all at once with axes and hammers.
