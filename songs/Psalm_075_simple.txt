Psalm 75

1 We thank you, God, we thank you. Your name is near, and your wonderful works show it.
2 When I gather the community, I will judge fairly.
3 The earth and all who live on it are falling apart, but I hold up its foundations. Selah.
4 I said to the foolish people, "Stop acting foolishly." And to the wicked, "Don't lift up your pride:"
5 Don't lift your pride high. Don't speak with a stubborn attitude.
6 Because success doesn't come from the east, west, or south.
7 But God is the judge. He brings one person down and lifts another up.
8 In the Lord's hand there is a cup of red mixed wine, and he pours it out. But the wicked of the earth will squeeze out the bitter leftovers and drink them.
9 But I will tell about you forever. I will sing praises to the God of Jacob.
10 I will cut off the pride of the wicked, but the righteous will be lifted up high.
