Psalm 144

1 The Lord is my strength, and I praise him. He teaches my hands to fight and my fingers to battle.
2 You are my good, my fortress, my high tower, my savior, my shield, and the one I trust. You help me keep my people in order.
3 Lord, what is a person that you notice him? Or a human that you care about him?
4 People are like nothing. Their days pass by like a shadow.
5 Lord, bend the skies and come down. Touch the mountains and make them smoke.
6 Send out lightning to scatter them. Shoot your arrows and destroy them.
7 Reach your hand from above. Rescue me from deep waters and from foreign people.
8 They speak empty words, and their right hand is a hand of lies.
9 I will sing a new song to you, God. I will praise you with a harp and a ten-string instrument.
10 He saves kings. He rescued his servant David from a harmful sword.
11 Rescue me from foreign people who speak empty words and whose right hand is a hand of lies.
12 May our sons grow strong like young plants. May our daughters be like polished cornerstones of a palace.
13 May our barns be full of all kinds of food. May our sheep multiply by thousands in our fields.
