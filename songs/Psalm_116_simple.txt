Psalm 116

1 I love the Lord because he has heard my voice and my prayers.
2 Because he has listened to me, I will pray to him as long as I live.
3 I was surrounded by the sadness of death, and the pains of the grave grabbed me. I found trouble and sorrow.
4 Then I prayed to the Lord: "O Lord, please save me."
5 The Lord is kind and fair. Yes, our God is full of mercy.
6 The Lord watches over those who are helpless. I was down, and he helped me.
7 Rest again, my soul, because the Lord has been good to you.
8 You saved my life from death, kept my eyes from crying, and my feet from slipping.
9 I will live in the presence of the Lord here in the land of the living.
10 I trusted, so I spoke. I was in great pain:
11 I said in my panic, "Everyone lies."
12 What can I give back to the Lord for all the good things he has done for me?
