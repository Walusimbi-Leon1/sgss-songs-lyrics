Psalm 123

1 I lift my eyes to you, who live in heaven.
2 Look, servants watch their boss's hand. A young woman watches her female boss's hand. In the same way, we watch the Lord our God until he shows us mercy.
3 Lord, show us mercy, show us mercy, because we are treated with so much disrespect.
4 We are full of the mocking of those who have it easy, and the disrespect of proud people.
