Psalm 130

1 I cry to you from the depths, Lord.
2 Lord, hear my voice. Please listen closely to my prayers.
3 If you, Lord, kept track of our sins, who could stand?
4 But with you there is forgiveness, so you can be respected.
5 I wait for the Lord. My whole being waits. I hope in his promises.
6 I wait for the Lord more than those who watch for morning. Yes, more than they wait for dawn.
7 Israel, hope in the Lord. With him there is mercy and abundant rescue.
8 He will rescue Israel from all their sins.
