Psalm 85

1 Lord, you have been kind to your land: you have brought back the people of Jacob from captivity.
2 You have forgiven your people's wrongdoing, you have covered over all their sins.
Selah.
3 You have taken away all your anger: you have turned away from being fiercely angry.
4 Bring us back, God our Savior, and stop being angry with us.
5 Will you stay angry with us forever? Will you keep your anger going for all generations?
6 Won't you give us new life again, so your people can be happy in you?
7 Show us your kindness, Lord, and give us your saving help.
8 I will listen to what God the Lord says, because he speaks peace to his people and to those who follow him. But they must not go back to foolish ways.
9 Surely his saving help is near those who honor him, so that glory can live in our land.
10 Kindness and truth have come together; doing right and peace have kissed each other.
11 Truth will grow up from the earth; and doing right will look down from heaven.
12 Yes, the Lord will give good things; and our land will produce its harvest.
13 Doing right will go before him; and will set us on the path of his footsteps.
