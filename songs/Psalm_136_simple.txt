Psalm 136

1 Thank the Lord, because he is good, and his mercy lasts forever.
2 Thank the God above all gods, because his mercy lasts forever.
3 Thank the Lord above all lords, because his mercy lasts forever.
4 Thank the one who alone does great wonders, because his mercy lasts forever.
5 Thank the one who made the skies with wisdom, because his mercy lasts forever.
6 Thank the one who spread the earth out over the waters, because his mercy lasts forever.
7 Thank the one who made the big lights, because his mercy lasts forever.
8 He made the sun to rule the day, because his mercy lasts forever.
9 He made the moon and stars to rule the night, because his mercy lasts forever.
10 Thank the one who struck Egypt and killed their firstborn, because his mercy lasts forever.
11 He brought Israel out from among them, because his mercy lasts forever.
12 He did it with a strong hand and an outstretched arm, because his mercy lasts forever.
13 Thank the one who split the Red Sea into parts, because his mercy lasts forever.
14 He let Israel pass through the middle of it, because his mercy lasts forever.
15 But he defeated Pharaoh and his army in the Red Sea, because his mercy lasts forever.
16 Thank the one who led his people through the desert, because his mercy lasts forever.
17 Thank the one who struck down great kings, because his mercy lasts forever.
18 He killed well-known kings, because his mercy lasts forever.
19 He struck Sihon king of the Amorites, because his mercy lasts forever.
20 And Og king of Bashan, because his mercy lasts forever.
21 He gave their land as an inheritance, because his mercy lasts forever.
22 That inheritance went to Israel, his servant, because his mercy lasts forever.
23 He remembered us when we were in a low place, because his mercy lasts forever.
24 He has saved us from our enemies, because his mercy lasts forever.
25 He gives food to every living thing, because his mercy lasts forever.
26 Thank the God of heaven, because his mercy lasts forever.
