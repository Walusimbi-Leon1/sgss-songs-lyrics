Psalm 38

2 Your arrows are stuck in me, and your hand is pressing me hard.
3 My body is all sore because you are angry. My bones can't rest because of my sin.
4 My sins are piled up over my head. They are a heavy load, too heavy for me to carry.
5 My wounds smell bad and are infected because I was foolish.
