Song of Solomon 1

1 This is the best song of all, written by Solomon.
2 I wish he would kiss me with his own lips, because your love is better than wine.
3 Your perfumes smell so good, and your name spreads like a sweet scent. That's why young women love you.
4 Pull me close, and we will run after you. The king has brought me into his rooms. We will be happy and full of joy in you. We will remember your love more than wine. Good people love you.
5 I am dark-skinned but beautiful, you women of Jerusalem. I am like the dark tents of Kedar, like Solomon's curtains.
6 Don't stare at me just because I'm dark; the sun has tanned me. My brothers and sisters were angry with me. They made me watch the vineyards, but I couldn't even take care of myself.
7 Please tell me, you whom I love so much, where you lead your sheep to eat. Where do you let them rest at noon? Why should I wander around like a stranger by my friends' sheep?
8 If you don't know, most beautiful woman, just follow the sheep's path. Feed your goats near the shepherds' tents.
9 My love, I compare you to a team of horses in Pharaoh's chariots.
10 Your cheeks are lovely with rows of jewels, and your neck with gold chains.
11 We will make you gold borders with little silver studs.
12 While the king sits at his table, my perfume sends out its smell.
13 My dear love is like a bundle of myrrh to me; he lies all night between my breasts.
14 My beloved is to me like a cluster of henna flowers in the vineyards of Engedi.
15 Look, you are beautiful, my love; look, you are beautiful. Your eyes are like doves.
16 Look, you are handsome, my beloved, and so pleasant. Our bed is green and fresh.
17 The beams of our house are cedar, and our rafters are fir.
