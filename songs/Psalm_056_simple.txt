Psalm 56

1 God, be kind to me, because people want to wipe me out. Someone fights me every day and pushes me down.
2 My enemies try to wipe me out every day. Many are fighting against me, O God Most High.
3 When I am scared, I will trust you.
4 I will praise God's word. I have put my trust in God. I will not fear what any person can do to me.
5 They twist my words every day. All their thoughts about me are evil.
6 They gather together and hide. They watch my steps, waiting to harm me.
7 Will they get away with their evil? God, in your anger, bring down those people.
8 You know all my wanderings. Put my tears in your bottle. Are they not in your book?
9 When I cry out to you, my enemies will turn back. I know this, because God is for me.
10 I will praise God's word. I will praise the Lord's word.
11 I have trusted in God. I will not be afraid of what people can do to me.
12 God, I have promised to praise you. I will give you thanks.
