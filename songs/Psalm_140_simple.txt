Psalm 140

1 Lord, rescue me from the evil person. Keep me safe from the violent person.
2 They dream up harm in their hearts. They are always gathering together for battle.
3 They have sharpened their tongues like a snake. The poison of a viper is under their lips. Selah.
4 Lord, keep me from the hands of the wicked. Protect me from the violent person who plans to trip me up.
5 The proud have hidden a trap and ropes for me. They spread a net by the roadside. They set traps for me. Selah.
6 I said to the Lord, "You are my God. Hear my cry for help, Lord."
7 Lord God, you are the power that saves me. You protected me on the day of battle.
8 Lord, don't give the wicked what they want. Don't help their evil plans, or they will boast. Selah.
9 For the leader of those who surround me, let the trouble from their own lips catch them.
10 May burning coals fall on them. May they be thrown into the fire and deep pits, never to rise again.
11 Don't let an evil talker get a foothold in the land. Trouble will chase the violent person to bring him down.
12 I know the Lord will stand up for the suffering and defend the rights of the poor.
13 Surely those who do right will thank you, Lord. The honest will live near you.
