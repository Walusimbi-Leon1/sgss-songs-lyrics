Psalm 5

1 Lord, listen to my words. Think about my quiet thoughts.
2 Listen to my cry for help, my King and my God. I will pray to you.
3 You will hear my voice in the morning, Lord. In the morning I will bring my prayer to you and look up to you.
4 You are not a God who likes evil. Evil cannot stay with you.
5 Foolish people cannot stand in your presence. You hate everyone who does wrong.
6 You will destroy those who tell lies. The Lord hates people who are violent and trick others.
7 But I will come into your house because of your great mercy. With respect for you, I will worship toward your holy temple.
8 Lead me, Lord, in your right ways because of my enemies. Show me a clear path ahead.
9 They don't speak truth. Their hearts are full of evil. Their mouths are like open graves. They use sweet words to trick.
10 God, destroy them. Let them be caught in their own plans. Throw them out because of all their sins, for they have turned against you.
11 But let all who trust you be happy. Let them always cheer because you protect them. Let those who love you be happy in you.
12 Lord, you will bless good people. You surround them with your love like a shield.
