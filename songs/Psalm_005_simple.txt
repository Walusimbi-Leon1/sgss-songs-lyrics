Psalm 5

1 Lord, listen to my words. Think about what I'm thinking.
2 Hear my cry for help, my King and my God, because I will pray to you.
3 Lord, you will hear my voice in the morning. I will pray to you in the morning and look up to you.
4 You are not a God who enjoys evil. Evil cannot live with you.
5 Foolish people cannot stand before you. You hate everyone who does wrong.
6 You will destroy those who tell lies. The Lord hates the violent and deceitful person.
7 But I will come to your house because you are so merciful. I will worship you with respect toward your holy temple.
8 Lord, lead me in your right ways because of my enemies. Show me clearly the path you want me to take.
9 They don't tell the truth. Their hearts are full of evil. Their mouths are like open graves. They use their tongues to say nice things just to trick people.
10 God, destroy them. Let them be caught by their own plans. Throw them out because of all their sins, because they have turned against you.
11 But everyone who trusts you should be happy. Let them always shout for joy because you protect them. Those who love your name should be glad in you.
12 Lord, you will bless the good people. You will surround them with your favor like a shield.
