Psalm 82

1 God stands in the community of the powerful; he judges among the gods.
2 How long will you judge unfairly, and take the side of the wicked? Selah.
3 Defend the poor and orphans. Make things right for the hurting and needy.
4 Save the poor and needy. Pull them out of the hands of the wicked.
5 They don't know, and they won't understand. They live in darkness. The whole world is falling apart.
6 I told you: You are gods, and all of you are children of the Highest.
7 But you will die like regular people, and fall like one of the leaders.
8 Get up, God, and judge the world, because you will inherit all nations.
