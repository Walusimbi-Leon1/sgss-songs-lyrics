Psalm 87

1 The Lord's city is built on the holy mountains.
2 The Lord loves the gates of Zion more than all the homes of Jacob's people.
3 People say wonderful things about you, city of God. Selah.
4 I will mention Rahab and Babylon to those who know me. Look, Philistia, Tyre, and Ethiopia—people were born there.
5 About Zion it will be said, "This one and that one were born there." The Highest God will make her strong.
6 When the Lord writes down the list of people, he will count them as born there. Selah.
7 Both singers and musicians will be there. All my life and joy come from you.
