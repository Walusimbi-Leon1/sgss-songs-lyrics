Psalm 1

1 A happy person is one who doesn't take advice from the wicked, doesn't hang out with sinners, and doesn't sit with those who make fun of others.
2 Instead, they love the Lord's teachings and think about them all day and night.
3 They are like a tree planted by a stream, that grows fruit at the right time. Its leaves don't dry up, and everything they do goes well.
4 The wicked are nothing like that. They are like dry husks the wind blows away.
5 So the wicked won't be able to stand in the judgment, and sinners won't be in the community of the righteous.
6 The Lord knows the path of the righteous, but the path of the wicked will be destroyed.
